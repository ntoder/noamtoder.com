About Brick Balls

A satisfying aim-and-shoot puzzle. Every turn a new row of numbered bricks appears at the top and the old ones move down. Aim and fire, and all your balls stream out one after another, bouncing off walls and bricks. Each hit takes one point off a brick, and at zero it breaks. Collect green rings to add balls to your stream. Where your first ball lands is where you shoot from next.

Tips

  • Low, angled shots off the side walls can reach far corners.
  • Always try to grab the green rings: more balls means more hits.
  • If a turn takes a long time, the balls speed up.

How to get better at Brick Balls

In Brick Balls, more balls is everything. Each green ring you collect adds one ball forever, so early on aim for the rings even if it means leaving some bricks.

The best shots get balls behind the bricks. If you can send a ball through a gap to the top, it bounces around up there, hitting bricks from above again and again. Look for a gap in the row nearest you and aim through it.

Watch the bottom row. The game ends when a brick reaches the red line, so when bricks get close, aim at them directly, even if it's less efficient. Bricks with big numbers can wait until they're closer.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Reach turn 20.
  • Getting good: Collect 30 balls.
  • Expert: Reach turn 60.

Did you know?

The angle a ball bounces off a wall is the same as the angle it hit at. Pool players and mini-golfers use this rule to plan bank shots.

Questions players ask

Where do I shoot from?

From wherever your first ball landed on the previous turn.

How do I get more balls?

Fly a ball through a green ring.
