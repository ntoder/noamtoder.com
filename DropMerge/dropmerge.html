About Drop Merge

A merging puzzle that mixes 2048 with a falling-block game. You drop numbered blocks into five columns. Whenever a block touches others with the same number, they combine into one block worth double (or more, if three or four join at once). Blocks fall to fill gaps, which can trigger chain reactions worth extra points. Plan where each block lands so the board doesn't fill up.

Tips

  • Three of the same touching merges into ×4!
  • Chain reactions multiply your points.
  • Check the next-next block to plan ahead.

How to get better at Drop Merge

Drop Merge is about keeping order. If you keep bigger numbers along the bottom and on one side, new small blocks will merge up into them step by step. A messy board with big and small numbers mixed together fills up fast.

Look at the next two blocks before you drop. If a 4 is coming after a 2, drop the 2 where it will make a 4 that can then meet the next block.

Chain reactions happen when a merge makes blocks fall onto matching numbers. Each step of a chain scores more, so setting them up on purpose is the way to big scores.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Make a 128 block.
  • Getting good: Make a 512 block.
  • Expert: Score 30,000 points.

Did you know?

Doubling grows really fast: start at 2 and double just 20 times and you're past one million.

Questions players ask

When does the game end?

When all five columns are full to the top.

What happens if three blocks match?

They all merge into one block worth four times as much.
