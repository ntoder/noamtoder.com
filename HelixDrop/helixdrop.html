About Helix Drop

A bouncy falling game. A ball bounces on a tall spiral tower made of rings, and every ring has a gap. Spin the tower so the gap lines up under the ball and it drops to the next ring. Red pieces end your run, but if you fall through three rings in a row, the ball becomes powerful enough to smash through anything. Reach the golden ring at the bottom to finish the level.

Tips

  • Watch for red pieces right next to the gap.
  • Falling through 3 rings in a row smashes the next one, even red!
  • Small, careful spins are safer than big ones.

How to get better at Helix Drop

Helix Drop is about timing your spins. The ball only checks the ring under it at the moment it lands, so you have the whole bounce to line up the next gap. Start turning as soon as the ball leaves a ring.

Red pieces are the danger. Before you spin a gap under the ball, check which pieces will pass under it on the way. Turning the long way around can be safer than going past a red piece.

Combos are your secret weapon. If you fall through three rings in a row without bouncing, the ball smashes the next ring whatever it's made of, red pieces included. Chaining falls is also worth more points.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Finish level 1.
  • Getting good: Reach level 4, where red pieces get more common.
  • Expert: Smash through a red ring with a combo.

Did you know?

A helix is a shape like a spring or a spiral staircase. DNA, the code inside living cells, is shaped like a double helix.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

Landing on a red piece, unless you're on a combo of three falls.

How do I finish a level?

Fall all the way to the golden ring at the bottom.
