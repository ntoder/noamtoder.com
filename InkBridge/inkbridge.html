Ink Bridge

A gentle slope is better than a steep one. A ball that's too fast bounces right out of the cup.

About Ink Bridge

A drawing physics puzzle. Each page of the notebook has a red ball, a golden cup and some grey walls. Draw your own lines with the pen: they become solid ramps, slides, bridges and bumpers. Press Go and gravity takes over. If the ball rolls into the cup, you win the page. Ink is limited, so short clever lines earn three stars. Eight levels to solve, and your progress is saved.

How to play

Draw with mouse / fingerMake a line
Space / ▶ GoDrop the ball (or reset it)
ZUndo last line

Tips

  • The ball must arrive slowly enough to stay in the cup.
  • You can draw while the ball is waiting, not while it's rolling.
  • Undo gives back the ink of that line.

How to get better at Ink Bridge

Ink Bridge is about slopes. A ramp tilted gently guides the ball where you want it. A steep ramp makes it fast, which is good for jumping gaps but bad for landing in the cup, because a fast ball bounces out.

Think about where the ball needs to end, then work backwards. The last line should drop the ball softly into the cup, the line before that should carry it there, and so on.

Fewer, shorter lines earn more stars. Once you've solved a level, try again with less ink. Sometimes one well-placed line is all you need.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve 3 levels.
  • Getting good: Get 3 stars on a level.
  • Expert: Solve all 8 levels.

Did you know?

Engineers who design roller coasters think about the same thing as this game: how slopes turn height into speed.

Questions players ask

The ball bounced out of the cup!

It was going too fast. Make the last slope gentler.

Can I change my lines?

Yes: press Undo line, or Clear to start the level over.
