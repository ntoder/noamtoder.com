About Idle Bakery

Build a cupcake empire! Tap the big cupcake to bake, then spend your cupcakes on helpers that bake for you: mixers, ovens, bakers, shops, factories and even a bakery on the Moon. Upgrades make everything faster, and golden stars give big bonuses. Your bakery saves by itself and keeps baking at half speed for up to two hours while you're away, so come back to see how much it has grown.

How to play

Click / tap the cupcakeBake
Click the shopBuy helpers and upgrades

Tips

  • Buy cheap helpers early. They pay for themselves quickly.
  • Upgrades that double a helper are often the best deal.
  • Catch the golden star when it appears for a frenzy or a big bonus.
