✨ What will it become?

When your pet becomes a Teen, it evolves depending on the games you've played most.

🐾 How it works

  • Every game you finish feeds your pet and gives it 10 XP. Your first game hatches the egg.
  • It grows from Baby to Kid, Teen, Grown-up and finally Legend.
  • As a Teen it evolves: play sports for Sporty, puzzles and brain games for Brainy, arcade games for Zappy, or word and creative games for Artsy.
  • Pat it and give it a treat (once an hour) to keep it happy. It gets a bit hungry while you're away, but it never gets sick or goes away.
  • Unlock accessories by earning achievements or by growing your pet.
  • Log in and your pet is saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices.
