About Color Hop

A tricky one-tap timing game. Your little ball bounces upward each time you tap. Spinning rings, sliding bars and turning crosses block the way, each split into four colours. You can only pass through the part that matches your ball's colour; touch any other colour and it's over. Every obstacle has a star in the middle to collect, and a colour wheel above it that changes your ball to a new random colour.

How to play

Tap, click or SpaceHop

Tips

  • Small, steady taps let you hover in place.
  • Watch the new colour after a colour wheel, then plan.
  • Falling off the bottom ends the game too.

How to get better at Color Hop

Color Hop is all about patience. You don't have to rush. Tap gently to hover just below an obstacle while it spins, and wait until your colour is at the bottom where you'll go through. Then hop twice quickly to get through before it turns.

Each obstacle has two danger points: the bottom edge, where you enter, and the top edge, where you leave. For rings, the same colour that's at the bottom when you enter is usually at the top by the time you leave only if you're fast. Short, quick double-hops work best.

Right after a colour wheel, your colour changes to something new. Stop and look before you go on. Many games end because players keep tapping out of habit.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Collect 5 stars.
  • Getting good: Collect 20 stars.
  • Expert: Collect 40 stars.

Did you know?

The four colours in each obstacle make a full circle of 360 degrees, so each colour covers exactly 90 degrees: a right angle.

Questions players ask

What does the colour wheel do?

It changes your ball to a different random colour.

What ends the game?

Touching a part of an obstacle that's a different colour, or falling off the bottom of the screen.
