About Ski Slope

A snowy downhill game inspired by an old computer classic. Your skier races down an endless mountain. Steer left and right around trees and rocks, ski through flag gates for points, and launch off ramps to fly over obstacles. Crashing slows you down. Once you pass 2,000 metres, a yeti comes charging after you, and it gets faster the further you go.

How to play

Tips

  • Skiing straight down is the fastest.
  • Ramps make you jump over everything for a moment.
  • Each flag gate in a row is worth more.

How to get better at Ski Slope

Ski Slope is about keeping your speed. Skiing straight down the hill is fastest, and every turn slows you. Look ahead and steer early with small taps, instead of last-second sharp turns that bleed speed.

Flag gates give lots of points, and each one is worth a bit more than the last. They get narrower the further you go. If a gate would force a sharp turn near a tree, skip it. A crash costs more than a missed gate.

After 2,000 metres the yeti appears and chases you. It's fast, but it can't jump, so blue ramps are your friend. Keep going as straight as you can, and don't crash, because a crash gives the yeti time to catch up.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Ski 1,000 metres.
  • Getting good: Pass 20 flag gates.
  • Expert: Escape the yeti for 500 metres.

Did you know?

Downhill ski racers can reach speeds of over 130 km/h, faster than cars on most motorways.

Questions players ask

What's the fastest way down?

Pointing straight downhill. Turning slows you.

Can I escape the yeti?

For a while! Stay fast and use ramps, but it gets faster the further you go.
