About Word Search

Find all the hidden words in the grid of letters. Words can run across, down and diagonally, and on hard they can even be written backwards. Drag across a word to circle it, and it gets crossed off your list. Choose a theme like animals, food, sports, space or colours, and try to find every word as fast as you can for a bigger time bonus.

How to play

Drag across lettersCircle a word

Tips

  • Look for unusual letters first, like Z, X or Q.
  • Scan one row at a time for the first letter of a word.
  • On hard, remember to check backwards too.
