About Burger Chef

A busy kitchen game. Hungry customers line up with their burger orders, each with a patience bar that slowly runs down. Build each burger from the bottom bun to the top bun, adding patties, cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles in exactly the order shown. A wrong ingredient sends the burger to the bin. Fast service earns tips, and the orders get bigger as the day gets busier.

How to get better at Burger Chef

Burger Chef is about reading and speed. Each order shows the finished burger. Build it from the bottom: always start with the bottom bun, then add each layer going up, and finish with the top bun. The list of ingredients on each order card also lists the middle layers from bottom to top.

Keep an eye on the patience bars. Green means happy, yellow means hurry, red means they're about to leave. Switch to the most impatient customer first, even if their burger is bigger.

On a keyboard, number keys 1 to 7 are much faster than clicking. With practice you can build a six-layer burger in two seconds. Tips depend on how much patience the customer had left, so speed really pays.
