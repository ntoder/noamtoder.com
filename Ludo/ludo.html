About Ludo

The classic race board game for two to four players. Each player has four tokens waiting in their coloured yard. Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the track, then race it all the way around the board and up your coloured home column to the centre. Land exactly on a rival's token to send it back to its yard, but watch out, because they can do the same to you. Star squares are safe. First to get all four tokens home wins.

Tips

  • Rolling a 6 or capturing gives you another turn.
  • Nobody can capture you on a ★ square or a start square.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre.

How to get better at Ludo

Ludo mixes luck with clever choices. Each roll often lets you move one of several tokens. Think about which move is safest and which one gets you closest to winning.

Capturing is powerful: it sends a rival token all the way back and gives you another roll. If a rival token is a few squares ahead of you, try to land on it. If a rival is a few squares behind one of your tokens, move that token away or onto a ★ safe square.

Having more than one token out gives you more choices each turn, so when you roll a 6 it's often worth bringing out a new token. Near the end, remember you need the exact number to land in the centre.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win a game against one computer player.
  • Getting good: Capture 3 tokens in one game.
  • Expert: Win a 4-player game.

Did you know?

Ludo comes from Pachisi, a game played in India for centuries. Emperor Akbar is said to have played it on a giant board in his palace courtyard, with real people as the pieces.

Questions players ask

How do I get a token out?

Roll a 6. Then pick the token in your yard.

What are the ★ squares?

Safe squares. A token there can't be captured.
