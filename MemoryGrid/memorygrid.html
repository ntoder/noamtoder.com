About Memory Grid

A visual memory game. A grid of squares appears and some of them flash blue. When they turn dark again, tap every square that was lit. Each level adds one more square to remember, and every few levels the grid grows, from 3×3 all the way to 7×7. A wrong tap costs one of your three lives and drops you back a level.

Tips

  • Group the squares into shapes or patterns.
  • Say the positions in your head: 'top-left, middle, bottom row'.
  • Losing a life takes you back one level, so you can try again.

How to get better at Memory Grid

Memory Grid tests your visual memory. Most people can hold about four to seven separate things in mind at once, so remembering ten single squares is very hard. The trick is to group them. Three squares in a row becomes one 'line'. A few squares in a corner becomes one 'clump'.

Try naming the pattern while it's still showing: 'a diagonal from the top-left, plus two in the bottom row'. Words and shapes stick better than dots.

There's no time limit when you're tapping, so take a moment to picture the pattern before you start. Tap the parts you're surest about first.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Reach level 5.
  • Getting good: Reach the 5×5 grid.
  • Expert: Reach level 15.

Did you know?

Memory champions use a trick called chunking: they group lots of small pieces of information into a few bigger pieces that are easier to remember.

Questions players ask

What happens if I tap a wrong square?

You lose a life, see where the squares were, and go back one level.

How big does the grid get?

It grows from 3×3 up to 7×7.
