About Slide Puzzle

The famous sliding tile puzzle. The numbered tiles are mixed up in a frame with one empty space, and you slide them around until they're back in order. Choose a 3 by 3 board for a quick game or 4 by 4 and 5 by 5 for a real challenge. Fewer moves and a faster time earn more points.

How to play

Click or tap a tileSlide it (and any tiles between it and the gap)
Arrow keysSlide a tile into the gap

Tips

  • Finish the top row, then the left column, then repeat on the smaller puzzle.
  • The last two tiles of a row are placed together.
  • Tap a tile in the gap's row or column to move several at once.

How to get better at Slide Puzzle

The trick to sliding puzzles is to solve them in layers. First put the whole top row in place, then the left column. What's left is a smaller puzzle, which you solve the same way, until only a 2 by 2 corner remains.

The hardest part is the end of each row. You can't just slide the last tile in, because that would knock the others out. Instead, line up the last two tiles of the row underneath their spots, then rotate them both into place together.

Don't panic if you seem to undo your progress for a moment. Moving a finished tile out of the way and back is often the only way forward.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve the 3 by 3 puzzle.
  • Getting good: Solve 4 by 4, the classic 15 puzzle.
  • Expert: Beat 5 by 5 in under 300 moves.

Did you know?

The 15 puzzle caused a worldwide craze in 1880. A famous prize was offered for solving an impossible version where two tiles were swapped, which can never be fixed by sliding.

Questions players ask

Can every puzzle be solved?

Yes, the tiles are shuffled by real slides, so it can always be undone.

How is my score worked out?

Bigger boards are worth more, minus points for every move and second.
