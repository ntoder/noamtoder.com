About Alien Invaders

A tribute to one of the first arcade games ever. Rows of pixel aliens march side to side, stepping down every time they reach the edge. The fewer aliens are left, the faster they move. Shoot them from your cannon, hide behind the shields, and don't let them reach the ground. Each new wave starts a little lower and drops more bombs.

How to play

Tips

  • You can only have one shot on screen at a time, so don't miss.
  • The top row is worth the most points.
  • Shields wear away, so don't stay behind one shield forever.

How to get better at Alien Invaders

In Alien Invaders the army moves as one block. It only steps down when its outer edge touches the side of the screen. If you clear out the left and right columns first, the block becomes narrower and has to walk further each time, which gives you more time before it reaches you.

You can only have one shot in the air (two from wave 4). A missed shot means waiting for it to fly off the top. Aim carefully, and fire from right under the alien you want instead of firing while moving.

The last few aliens move very fast. Stay in the middle so you can reach either side, and lead your shots a little so the alien walks into them. When the red saucer flies across the top, it's worth a lot of points.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear wave 1.
  • Getting good: Reach wave 3.
  • Expert: Hit the mystery saucer.

Did you know?

The original alien-invasion arcade game came out in Japan in 1978. Its aliens sped up as you destroyed them because the computer had less to draw, and the designer kept that as a feature.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

Losing all three lives, or the aliens reaching the ground.

How much is the saucer worth?

It's a mystery: 50, 100, 150 or 300 points.
