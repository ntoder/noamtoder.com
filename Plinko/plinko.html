About Plinko

A bouncy luck-and-aim game inspired by a famous TV game show board. Drop a ball from the top and watch it bounce off rows of pegs, zig-zagging down into one of the scoring slots. The outside slots are worth the most but are hard to reach, and the middle has some zeros. A gold peg doubles the points of any ball that touches it.

Tips

  • Drop close to the walls to aim for the 100 slots.
  • The middle has the 50 slot, but zeros right next to it.
  • Every ball that touches the gold peg turns gold and scores double.

How to get better at Plinko

Plinko is mostly luck, but aim matters. A ball usually lands somewhere near the spot you dropped it, with a random spread of a few slots either way. Dropping near the left or right wall gives you a real chance at the 100 slots, and the wall stops it bouncing too far out.

The middle is a gamble: the 50 slot sits between two zeros. If you want steady points, aim for the area between the edge and the middle, where the 10s and 25s are.

Keep an eye on the gold peg. It moves to a new place every game. Dropping right above it gives the best chance to turn your ball gold and double its score.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Score 200 points.
  • Getting good: Land a ball in a 100 slot.
  • Expert: Score 600 points in one game.

Did you know?

When lots of balls fall through a peg board, most land in the middle and fewer reach the edges. Scientists call this shape a bell curve, and a peg board that shows it is called a Galton board.
