About Word Guess

Guess the secret five-letter English word in six tries. After each guess the letters change colour: green means the letter is in the right place, yellow means it's in the word but somewhere else, and grey means it isn't in the word at all. Use the clues to narrow it down. Play today's word, which is the same for everyone, or as many random words as you like, and build a winning streak.

How to play

Type or tap lettersSpell your guess
EnterCheck your guess
BackspaceDelete a letter

Tips

  • Start with a word that has lots of different common letters, like CRANE or SLATE.
  • Don't reuse grey letters. They aren't in the word.
  • Remember a yellow letter must move to a different spot.
