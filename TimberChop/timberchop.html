Timber Chop

Tap the left or right side of the screen (or ⬅ ➡) to chop from that side. The tree slides down after every chop.

About Timber Chop

A fast one-button-per-side reflex game. You're a lumberjack chopping a never-ending tree. Each chop knocks out the bottom piece and the whole tree drops down. Branches stick out on the left and right, so if one is about to land on your side, jump to the other side before you chop. A timer bar drains constantly and each chop tops it up, so you have to keep chopping faster and faster.

How to play

⬅ / A or tap leftChop from the left
➡ / D or tap rightChop from the right

Tips

  • Watch the second piece up, not the bottom one.
  • The timer drains faster as your score grows.
  • Find a rhythm: left-left-right…

How to get better at Timber Chop

Timber Chop is all about reading one piece ahead. The bottom piece is what you're chopping now, but the piece above it is what will drop onto your level next. If that piece has a branch on your side, switch sides with this chop.

The timer forces you to speed up. Early on there's lots of time, so practise reading ahead. Later the bar drains quickly and you'll need to chop several times a second.

Branches never appear on both sides of the same piece, so there's always a safe side. Trust your eyes and keep a steady rhythm.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Chop 50 pieces.
  • Getting good: Chop 150 pieces.
  • Expert: Chop 300 pieces.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

A branch landing on you, or the timer bar running out.

Why does the timer drain faster?

It speeds up as your score grows, to keep you on your toes.
