About Dodge!

A pure survival game. You're a little glowing dot in an arena, and everything else wants to hit you. Pink balls fly in and bounce off the walls, big purple balls are slow but huge, orange stars hunt you down, and laser lines flash a warning before they blast across the screen. Dash to zip through gaps, grab stars to clear the area around you, and pick up shields. The longer you last, the faster it gets.

How to play

Arrows / WASD or dragMove
Space / Shift or double-tapDash

Tips

  • Dashing makes you invincible for a split second.
  • Orange stars follow you, so lead them into other balls' paths... or just outrun them.
  • When a laser line flashes, get off it fast.

How to get better at Dodge!

Dodge! is about space and calm. Panicking makes you run into things. Keep to the open middle of the arena, make small movements, and watch the edges for new balls flying in.

Your dash is a get-out-of-jail card: for a split second nothing can hit you. It recharges in 1.5 seconds, so use it when you're about to be hit, not just to move faster.

Lasers always flash a warning line first. As soon as you see one, step off it. Stars clear every ball close to you, so grab them when it's crowded. Shields absorb one hit.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Survive 30 seconds.
  • Getting good: Survive 60 seconds.
  • Expert: Survive 90 seconds.

Did you know?

Games where you dodge huge numbers of bullets are called 'bullet hell' games. Some have thousands of bullets on screen at once.

Questions players ask

How do I dash?

Press Space or Shift, or double-tap the screen. It recharges in 1.5 seconds.

Does it end?

No. It keeps getting harder until you're hit.
