City Defender

Rockets are falling on your six cities! Tap or click anywhere in the sky to launch a missile there. It explodes and stops any rocket inside the blast.

About City Defender

A sky-defence game inspired by an arcade classic. Enemy rockets streak down toward six cities. Tap a spot in the sky and the nearest launcher fires a missile there. When it arrives it makes a big explosion, and any rocket that flies into it is destroyed. Every wave brings more and faster rockets, and some split into several. Surviving cities and leftover missiles earn bonus points.

How to play

Tap or click the skyLaunch a missile there

Tips

  • One explosion can catch several rockets. Wait for them to bunch up.
  • Don't waste missiles: you only get 30 per wave.
  • Every third wave rebuilds one lost city.

How to get better at City Defender

City Defender is about leading your shots. Your missile takes a moment to fly up, and the rocket keeps falling while it does. Aim below the rocket along its trail, at the point it will reach when your missile arrives.

Explosions stay in the sky for about a second. Try to place one where several rocket trails cross, so a single missile takes out two or three. That saves ammo for the later, busier part of the wave.

Watch for the rockets that split. If you catch them high up, before they split, you only need one missile instead of three. Your launchers are targets too, so protect the middle one, which can reach both sides.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Survive wave 3.
  • Getting good: Finish a wave without losing a city.
  • Expert: Reach wave 8.

Did you know?

Real air-defence systems work out where a fast object will be in the future, using its speed and direction. Your brain does the same thing when you catch a ball.

Questions players ask

What happens when a launcher is hit?

It loses all its missiles for the rest of that wave.

Can I get cities back?

Yes. Every third wave one destroyed city is rebuilt.
