About Maze Escape

Race through a brand new maze every time and find the exit before the clock runs out. Each maze you escape adds time to the clock, and the next one is a little bigger. Pick up coins along the way for points and extra seconds. For a real challenge, try the dark mode, where you can only see the area right around you, but every exit is worth more.

How to play

Arrow keys / WASDMove
Hold a keyKeep walking
SwipeMove on a touch screen

Tips

  • Follow one wall with your hand. It always leads somewhere.
  • Coins give extra time, so grab the ones that are close.
  • Your trail shows where you have already been.
