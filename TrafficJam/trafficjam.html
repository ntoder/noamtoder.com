Traffic Jam

The red car is stuck! Drag the cars forward and backward (they can only move in the direction they face) to clear a path to the exit on the right.
Every level is a new puzzle.

About Traffic Jam

A sliding-car puzzle in a crowded parking lot. Cars and trucks can only drive forward or backward, and your goal is to clear a path so the red car can drive out of the exit. Every level is freshly made and checked by the computer, which also tells you the fewest moves it can be solved in.

How to play

Drag a carSlide it along its lane

Tips

  • Find the cars blocking the red car first.
  • Long trucks are usually the key pieces.
  • Fewer moves earn more points.

How to get better at Traffic Jam

The best way to solve a Traffic Jam puzzle is to work backward from the goal. Look at the red car's row and find every car blocking it. For each blocker, ask: which way does it need to move, and what's in its way? Keep going until you find a car that can move freely, and start there.

Vertical cars in the red car's row usually need to move up or down by a whole space, which means the cars above or below them must move first. Trucks, which are three spaces long, are the hardest to shift and are often the real problem.

The level tells you the fewest moves possible. A move is sliding one car any distance, so try to move each car straight to where it needs to be.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve the first five levels.
  • Getting good: Solve a level in exactly the fewest moves.
  • Expert: Reach level 15.

Did you know?

Sliding-block car puzzles became famous in the 1990s, but the idea goes back to the 1930s. Computer scientists have shown that very large versions of this puzzle are extremely hard for computers to solve.

Questions players ask

What counts as a move?

Sliding one car, any number of spaces.

Is every level solvable?

Yes, the computer solves every level before showing it to you.
