About Pipe Connect

A relaxing plumbing puzzle. Every tile has a piece of pipe, and they're all twisted the wrong way. Tap a tile to rotate it a quarter turn. When pipes line up, water flows from the source in the middle and lights them up blue. Connect the whole grid to finish the level. The grid grows from 4 by 4 up to 8 by 8.

How to play

Click or tap a tileTurn it a quarter turn

Tips

  • Corner tiles can only connect two ways, so fix them first.
  • Dead ends (a single short pipe) must point at a neighbour.
  • Straight pipes on an edge must run along that edge.

How to get better at Pipe Connect

Every puzzle has exactly one solution where all the pipes connect, and the edges give it away. A pipe on the edge of the grid can never point outward, so edge and corner tiles have very few possible positions. Fix those first.

Next look at the dead ends, the tiles with just one short pipe stub. Each one must point at a neighbour that has an opening facing back. Once a tile is certainly right, you can use it as a clue for its neighbours.

The water shows your progress: blue pipes are already connected to the source. If a big area won't fill, look for the one tile where the water stops.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve the 4 by 4 and 5 by 5 grids.
  • Getting good: Solve 6 by 6 in fewer taps than tiles times two.
  • Expert: Finish the 8 by 8 grid.

Did you know?

Real water pipes in houses use the same idea: every pipe joint must line up perfectly, or water leaks out. Plumbers plan the whole network of pipes before they start building.

Questions players ask

Is there always a solution?

Yes, every grid is made from a connected network, then the tiles are turned.

Do taps matter?

Finishing in fewer taps gives a bigger bonus.
