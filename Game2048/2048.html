About 2048

2048 is a sliding number puzzle. Every move pushes all the tiles in one direction, and two tiles with the same number join into one with double the value. A new tile appears after every move. Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score.

How to play

Arrow keys / WASDSlide the tiles
PhoneSwipe
UndoTake back your last move

Tips

  • Pick a corner and keep your biggest tile there.
  • Avoid the direction that pulls your big tile out of its corner.
  • Build a chain of numbers that get smaller as they move away from the corner.
