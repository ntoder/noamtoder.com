About Flag Quiz

How well do you know the world? Name the country from its flag, find the right flag for a country, or match countries with their capital cities. Each round has ten questions with ten seconds each, and quicker answers score more points. Play the whole world or focus on one region such as Europe, Asia, the Americas or Africa. A great way to learn geography.

Tips

  • Look at the colours and symbols. Many neighbouring countries have similar flags.
  • Answer quickly but carefully. Three right in a row doubles your points.
  • Practise one region at a time to learn it well.
