About Spot the Difference

A classic eye-test puzzle. Two picture panels sit side by side, painted fresh every round. They look the same, but five things in the right picture are different: something is missing, swapped for something else, flipped, bigger, moved, or a different colour. Find all five to move to the next round, where the pictures get busier and the clock gets shorter. Every second left over becomes bonus points.

Tips

  • Compare one small area at a time.
  • Look for things that face the other way.
  • Guessing costs 3 seconds, so be sure.

How to get better at Spot the Difference

Spot the Difference is easier with a system. Instead of looking at the whole picture, compare small strips: the top row of both pictures, then the next, and so on. Your eyes notice changes much better in a small area.

Learn the six kinds of change: something missing, something swapped for a different object, something flipped to face the other way, something bigger, something moved up or down, and something in a different colour. When you know what to look for, they jump out.

Wrong taps cost three seconds, so don't tap randomly. And because leftover time becomes bonus points, finishing quickly really pays off.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear round 1.
  • Getting good: Clear round 3.
  • Expert: Score 4,000 points.

Did you know?

Spot-the-difference puzzles work because of something called change blindness: our brains are surprisingly bad at noticing changes unless we're looking right at them.
