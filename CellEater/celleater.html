About Cell Eater

A survival-of-the-biggest game. You're a small cell in a big arena full of food dots and other hungry cells. Eat dots to grow, and once you're noticeably bigger than another cell you can swallow it whole. But the bigger you get, the slower you move, and there's always someone bigger out there. Climb the top-five leaderboard and become the biggest cell in the dish.

How to play

Mouse or dragMove

Tips

  • You need to be about 15% bigger than a cell to eat it.
  • Big cells slowly shrink, so keep eating.
  • Use the boost to catch a cell that's running away, or to escape.

How to get better at Cell Eater

In Cell Eater, size is power, but speed is life. Small cells are quick and can dodge almost anything. Spend your early game safely eating dots, and only chase cells that are clearly smaller than you.

You can only swallow a cell if you're about 15% bigger, and you have to cover most of it. Corner small cells against the edge of the arena so they can't escape. The speed boost helps close the last gap, but it costs mass, so use it only when you're sure.

Once you're big, you're slow, and you slowly lose mass over time. Watch the leaderboard: if someone is bigger than you, stay away from them and keep eating. The minimap in the corner shows where you are in the arena.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Reach 100 mass.
  • Getting good: Eat 5 other cells.
  • Expert: Be #1 on the leaderboard.

Did you know?

Real single-celled creatures called amoebas eat by flowing around their food and swallowing it, a bit like the cells in this game.

Questions players ask

How do I eat another cell?

Be about 15% bigger than it and move over it.

Why am I getting smaller?

All cells slowly lose mass over time, and boosting costs mass too.
