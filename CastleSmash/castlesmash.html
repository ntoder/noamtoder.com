About Castle Smash

A physics demolition game. Goblins have built little castles out of glass, wood and stone, and you have a slingshot and a few stones. Drag back to aim, and the dotted line shows the start of the stone's path. Smash the blocks so the castle collapses, and knock out every goblin to win the level. Goblins are knocked out by a direct hit, by a falling block, or by falling from a height. Five castles to conquer.

How to play

Drag back and let goFling a stone

Tips

  • Glass breaks in one hit, wood in two, stone in four.
  • Knocking out supports brings the whole tower down.
  • Each stone you don't use is worth 200 bonus points.

How to get better at Castle Smash

Castle Smash is about finding the weak point. Instead of aiming straight at a goblin, look at what's holding it up. Breaking a supporting pillar makes everything above it fall, and falling blocks knock out goblins below and around them.

Stronger shots break more. A stone flying fast does much more damage than a slow one, so pull back far for heavy materials like stone. A gentle lob is enough to shatter glass.

You have a limited number of stones, and each one you save is worth 200 points. Take your time to line up every shot using the dotted path.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear the first castle.
  • Getting good: Clear a castle with one stone.
  • Expert: Smash all 5 castles.

Did you know?

Real catapults and trebuchets were used for hundreds of years to attack castles. The biggest trebuchets could throw stones heavier than a car.

Questions players ask

How do I aim?

Press anywhere, drag away from the castle, and let go. Further = stronger.

How do I knock out a goblin?

Hit it with a stone, drop a block on it, or make it fall from high up.
