About Zombie Survival

A top-down survival shooter with silly green zombies. They shuffle in from every edge of the screen, and each wave brings more of them, with fast runners and big tough ones mixed in. Move with the keyboard, aim and shoot with the mouse, and grab power-ups for a machine gun, a shotgun or extra health. You recover a little health between waves. How many waves can you survive?

  • Keep moving: zombies are slower than you.

  • The shotgun is great when they get close.

How to get better at Zombie Survival

The golden rule in Zombie Survival is to keep moving. You're faster than ordinary zombies, so walking in a big circle around the edge of the arena keeps them bunched up behind you, where you can shoot them in a line.

Watch out for the fast, pale-green runners: shoot them first, because they catch up quickly. Big dark zombies are slow but tough. They take lots of hits, but they nearly always drop a power-up.

The machine gun and shotgun have limited ammo, then you go back to the pistol. Save the shotgun for close moments, when a crowd is about to reach you. Grab ❤️ drops when your health bar turns red.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Survive wave 3.
  • Getting good: Defeat 100 zombies.
  • Expert: Reach wave 10.

Did you know?

Zombies in films are usually slow because the first zombie movies showed them shuffling. Fast zombies only became common in films much later.

Questions players ask

How do I get better weapons?

Shoot zombies; sometimes they drop a 🔫 or 💥. Big zombies almost always do.

Do I get health back?

A little between waves, and a lot from ❤️ drops.
