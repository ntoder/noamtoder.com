Word Guess strategy: the best first words and how to think

By Noam · 15 November 2026 · 6 min read

Word Guess gives you six tries to find a secret five-letter word. Some days you get it in three; some days you scrape through on the last line. Luck plays a part, but good players lose much less often. Here's how they think.

Choose a strong first word

Your first guess is the only one where you know nothing, so its job is to collect as much information as possible. That means five different letters, chosen from the most common letters in English words. E, A, R, T, O, I, L, S and N appear in a huge number of five-letter words. Words like CRANE, SLATE, STARE and RAISE test several of them at once.

Avoid double letters in your opener, because a repeated letter wastes a slot. Avoid rare letters like Q, J, X and Z too. They're exciting when they hit, but they almost never do.

Read the colours carefully

  • Green means the letter is in the word and in that exact spot. Keep it there.
  • Yellow means the letter is in the word but not in that spot. This is two clues: the letter is in, and it isn't there. Move it somewhere else next time.
  • Grey means the letter isn't in the word. Never use it again.

The most common beginner mistake is putting a yellow letter back in the same position. It's already told you that spot is wrong.

Don't forget repeated letters

Answers can contain the same letter twice, like SPEED or LLAMA. If you've found most of the letters but can't make a word, consider a double. If you guess a word with two Es and only one turns yellow or green, the answer has just one E.

When many words are possible, test letters instead

Imagine you've found _IGHT. The answer could be LIGHT, MIGHT, NIGHT, RIGHT, SIGHT, TIGHT, FIGHT or WIGHT. If you guess them one at a time, you might run out of tries. Instead, play a word that tests several of those first letters at once, like FLIRT or MONTH, even though you know it isn't the answer. Whatever lights up tells you which of the eight it is, and you win on the next guess.

This is the biggest difference between beginners and experts: experts don't always guess a word that could be right. They guess the word that teaches them the most.

Think about where letters usually go

Some letters prefer certain positions. S is very common at the start of words. E and Y are common at the end. Letter pairs like CH, SH, TH and ST show up all the time. When you have a yellow letter, try it in the position where it's most likely to be.

Daily or random?

In our Word Guess game, today's word is the same for every player, so you can compare with friends. Random mode gives you as many new words as you like, which is perfect for practising these techniques. Win in a row to build a streak, and the fewer guesses you need, the more points you get.
