Shape Shifter

Walls fly toward you, and each has a hole in a certain shape. Become that shape and move to that row before the wall hits you!
Keys: ⬆ ⬇ change row, 1 2 3 (or Z X C) pick ● ■ ▲, Space switches to the next shape. Touch: tap the left side to change shape, the right side to move up or down. After 30 walls, ★ joins the party!

Game Over

Look at the colour of the hole's outline: it's the same colour as the shape you need.

About Shape Shifter

A fast thinking-and-reflex game. You're a glowing shape that can change form in an instant: circle, square or triangle, and later a star. Walls rush in from the right, each with a single hole in one of three rows. Match the shape AND the row before the wall reaches you, then do it again, faster and faster. The colour of each hole matches the shape it needs, which helps when things get hectic.

Tips

  • Hole colours match shape colours.
  • Change shape first, then row.
  • Walls get faster and closer together.

How to get better at Shape Shifter

Shape Shifter asks you to do two things for every wall: pick the right shape and move to the right row. Do the shape first, because you can see it from far away, then slide into the row when the wall gets closer.

Use the colours. Each shape has its own colour (circle red, square green, triangle yellow, star purple), and each hole's outline uses that colour. With practice you'll react to the colour before you even think about the shape.

On a keyboard, keep your left hand on 1-2-3 (or Z-X-C) and your right hand on the arrow keys. Then you can change shape and row at the same time.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Pass 20 walls.
  • Getting good: Unlock the star shape (30 walls).
  • Expert: Pass 80 walls.

Did you know?

Some TV game shows have used real walls with cut-out shapes that contestants must squeeze through before being pushed into a pool.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

Hitting a wall with the wrong shape or in the wrong row.

When does the star appear?

After 30 seconds.
