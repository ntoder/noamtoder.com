About Jetpack Dash

An endless flying game set in a secret lab. Your hero runs forward on their own, and you control just one thing: the jetpack. Hold to rise, let go to fall. Electric zappers block the way at different angles, some of them spinning, and homing missiles are announced by a flashing warning. Collect coins and see how far you can fly before you get zapped.

Tips

  • Quick taps let you hover at one height.
  • When the ❗ appears, the missile aims at your height. Change it at the last moment.
  • Following coin lines is often a safe path.

How to get better at Jetpack Dash

Jetpack Dash is about controlling your height. Holding the button sends you up fast and letting go drops you fast, so the real skill is tapping in a rhythm to hover. Practise staying in the middle of the screen, where you can dodge up or down.

Zappers come at four angles. Flat ones are easy to fly over or under. Diagonal ones need you to be at the right height early, and spinning ones need timing, so watch them turn before you commit.

Missiles lock onto your height while the warning flashes. Don't move away too early, or the missile follows you. Wait until the warning stops, then change height quickly.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Fly 500 metres.
  • Getting good: Collect 100 coins in one run.
  • Expert: Fly 2,000 metres.

Did you know?

Real jetpacks exist! The first one was tested in the 1960s, but it could only fly for about 20 seconds before running out of fuel.

Questions players ask

How do I stay in the air?

Tap the button quickly over and over instead of holding it.

What does the red ❗ mean?

A missile is about to fly in at that height.
