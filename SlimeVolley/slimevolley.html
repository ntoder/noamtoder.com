About Slime Volley

A bouncy volleyball game with two blobby slimes. Each slime guards its half of the beach. Move under the ball and bump it back over the net: where it touches your slime decides which way it flies, and moving or jumping as you hit it adds power. If the ball touches the sand on your side, your opponent scores. First to seven wins. Play against the computer or a friend on the same keyboard.

Tips

  • Hit the ball with the front of your slime to send it over.
  • Jumping into the ball near the net makes a hard spike.
  • Stay near the middle of your half so you can reach anywhere.

How to get better at Slime Volley

Slime Volley is all about where the ball touches your slime. Your slime is a half-circle, so the ball bounces off at an angle: hit it with the side facing the net to send it forward, or with the top to pop it straight up.

Moving while you hit adds speed. Running toward the net as the ball lands on you gives a flatter, faster shot that's hard to return. Jumping near the net and hitting the ball as it comes over is a spike.

Positioning wins points. Get back to the middle of your half after each hit, and watch the ball's shadow on the sand: it shows where the ball is going to land.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Score your first point.
  • Getting good: Beat the computer.
  • Expert: Win 7–0!

Did you know?

Beach volleyball became an Olympic sport in 1996. Teams have just two players, compared with six in indoor volleyball.

Yes, it bounces off the net and the top of the net.
