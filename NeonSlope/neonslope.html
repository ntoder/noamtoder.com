About Neon Slope

A fast 3D-style runner in glowing neon. Your ball rolls down an endless track that stretches into the distance. Rows of blocks block some lanes, and every so often there's a gap in the track that you must jump. Collect diamonds along the way. The ball speeds up the further you go, and the colours shift as you travel deeper.

How to play

⬅ ➡ or A DSteer
Space or ⬆Jump

Tips

  • Jumping clears blocks too, but you can't steer much in the air.
  • Gaps come every so often, so always be ready to jump.
  • Each 💎 is worth 25 points.

How to get better at Neon Slope

Neon Slope is about looking ahead. Blocks appear far down the track and grow as they get closer. Pick the open lane early and drift into it smoothly instead of swerving at the last moment.

Gaps in the track can only be crossed by jumping. Time your jump so you take off just before the gap. A jump also carries you over blocks, which is a handy escape when a wall of blocks leaves only a hard-to-reach lane.

The speed keeps rising, so small movements matter more and more. Stay near the middle when you can, because from there every lane is close.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Score 200 points.
  • Getting good: Collect 10 gems in one run.
  • Expert: Score 1,000 points.

Did you know?

Neon signs glow because electricity passes through a gas inside a glass tube. Pure neon glows red-orange; other colours come from other gases or coatings.

Questions players ask

How do I cross the gaps?

Jump with Space, ⬆ or the ⤒ button just before you reach them.

Does it end?

No, the track is endless and keeps getting faster.
