About Bubble Pop

A classic bubble shooter. Aim the launcher, bounce shots off the walls, and match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. Any bubbles left hanging with nothing holding them up fall off for bonus points. Every few misses the whole ceiling drops a row, so plan your shots before the bubbles reach the line. Clear the board to move to the next level, where more colors join in.

Tips

  • Aim at the bubbles holding up a cluster. Everything below them falls for bonus points.
  • Bounce off the walls to reach bubbles behind others.
  • Pops in a row build a combo, which multiplies your points.
  • Save a bomb or rainbow bubble for when the bubbles get close to the line.
  • Swap to the next bubble if it fits better.

How to get better at Bubble Pop

The biggest scores in Bubble Pop don't come from popping, they come from dropping. When you pop a group that's holding other bubbles up, everything hanging underneath falls and is worth more than a pop. Before each shot, look for a small group near the top that connects to a big cluster below it.

Use the walls. The dotted aim line shows one or two bounces, which lets you reach gaps the launcher can't hit straight. A bank shot that slides into a narrow gap is often the only way to reach a buried colour.

Keep an eye on the yellow dots next to the launcher: each miss uses one, and when they run out the ceiling drops a row. If a shot won't pop anything, place the bubble where it builds a group for later instead of throwing it away. Swap to your next bubble (Down arrow, or tap it) when it fits better.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Early levels use only three or four colours. Practise bouncing shots off the walls.
  • Getting good: From level 3, more colours join in. Look for shots that drop whole clusters.
  • Expert: Later levels start with more rows and fewer shots before the ceiling drops. Save bombs and rainbow bubbles for emergencies.

Did you know?

Bubble-shooting puzzle games became popular in arcades in the mid-1990s, and the idea of bouncing shots off the side walls has been part of the genre ever since.

Questions players ask

Why did the ceiling move down?

Every shot that doesn't pop anything uses up one of the yellow dots. When they're all gone, the ceiling drops a row.

What do the special bubbles do?

A bomb blasts every bubble around where it lands. A rainbow bubble matches any colour.

How do I lose?

If the bubbles cross the dashed line near the bottom, the game ends.

Can I change which bubble I shoot?

Yes. Press the Down arrow or tap the 'Next' bubble to swap them.
