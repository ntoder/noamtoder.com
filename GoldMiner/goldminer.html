About Gold Miner

A classic grabbing game. Your miner has a swinging claw on a long rope. Drop it at the right moment and it shoots down, grabbing whatever it touches: gold nuggets, sparkling diamonds, worthless rocks, old bones, or mystery bags. Heavy things take a long time to reel in, which wastes precious seconds, so choose your targets carefully. Collect enough money before the timer runs out to reach the next, harder level.

How to play

Tap / Space / ⬇Drop the claw
Tap again / ⬆ / XUse dynamite

Tips

  • Big gold = big money, but slow to pull.
  • Use dynamite on rocks you grab by mistake.
  • Mystery bags can give money, dynamite or super strength.

How to get better at Gold Miner

Gold Miner is about value per second. Every grab costs time, and heavy things cost a lot more. A big gold nugget is worth it, but a big rock wastes ten seconds for almost nothing. Wait for the claw to swing to a good target before you drop it.

Diamonds are the best treasure: tiny, light and worth the most. Small gold is quick but cheap. Mystery bags are a gamble, but they can give extra dynamite or a strength boost that makes you reel everything in faster.

If you grab a rock, use dynamite to blow it up and get the claw back quickly. You start with one stick, and bags can give you more.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Reach level 3.
  • Getting good: Grab a diamond.
  • Expert: Reach level 6.

Questions players ask

How do I use dynamite?

While the claw is pulling something up, tap again or press ⬆. It destroys what you're holding.

What happens when time runs out?

If you've reached the target you go to the next level. Otherwise the game ends.
