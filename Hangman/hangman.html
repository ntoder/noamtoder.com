About Balloon Hangman

A friendly version of the classic word-guessing game. Instead of drawing a gallows, your basket is held up by seven balloons, and each wrong guess pops one. The words come from fun categories like animals, food, sports and space, and the category is shown to help you. Guess words in a row to build a streak multiplier.

How to get better at Balloon Hangman

Good hangman players don't guess at random. In English, the most common letters are E, A, R, I, O, T, N and S, so those are the safest first guesses. Vowels are especially useful, because almost every word has at least one and they show you the shape of the word.

Once some letters are showing, look at the pattern. A word like _ _ _ T _ _ _ in the Animals category with two vowels showing might jump out at you. Think about the category first, then about which words fit the length and letters.

Watch for double letters and common endings like -ING, -ER and -ION. If you see the start of a common ending, fill it in.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Guess your first word.
  • Getting good: Get five words in a row.
  • Expert: Build a streak of ten words.

Did you know?

Hangman has been played on paper for well over a hundred years. The letter E is the most used letter in English and appears in about one in every eight letters you read.
