About Moon Lander

A gentle physics challenge. Your lander is falling toward the Moon, pulled down by gravity. Fire the engine to slow down, and tilt to move sideways. To land safely you must touch down on a flat green pad going slowly, standing straight and not drifting. Each landing gives points for the pad's multiplier and leftover fuel, then the next level has rougher ground and less fuel.

How to play

⬆ W or SpaceFire the engine
⬅ ➡ or A DTilt the lander

Tips

  • Stop sliding sideways first, then worry about falling.
  • Tilt back to straight before you touch down.
  • The x2 and x3 pads are worth the risk if you have fuel.

How to get better at Moon Lander

Moon Lander is all about speed control. Gravity pulls you down a little faster every second, so it's easier to keep your speed low the whole way than to brake hard at the end. Give the engine short taps to hover your falling speed around 15 to 20.

To move sideways, tilt the lander and fire. Then tilt the other way and fire again to stop. The three numbers in the corner show your falling speed, sideways speed and tilt. All three must be green when your legs touch the pad.

Pick your pad early. Smaller pads have bigger multipliers, but they need more precise flying. If your fuel is getting low, go for the nearest pad, because a crash ends the game but a landing refills some fuel.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Land once.
  • Getting good: Land on a x3 pad.
  • Expert: Reach level 5.

Did you know?

The Moon's gravity is about one sixth of Earth's. That's why astronauts could bounce around, and why a lander only needs a small engine to hover.

Questions players ask

Why did I crash on the pad?

One of the three numbers was red: falling too fast, sliding sideways, or tilted.

What happens when I run out of fuel?

The engine stops working and you just fall, so be careful!
