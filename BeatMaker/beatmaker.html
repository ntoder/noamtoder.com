About Beat Maker

A music toy for making beats. The grid has eight instruments, from kick drum and snare to bass, bells and chords, and sixteen steps that loop round and round. Switch squares on and off to build a rhythm and a tune, change the speed with the tempo slider, and add swing for a bouncy groove. Load a preset to get started, and copy a link to share your beat with friends.

How to get better at Beat Maker

Beat Maker works like a real drum machine. The 16 columns are steps, one bar of music, and the playhead moves left to right in a loop. Every square you switch on plays when the playhead reaches it.

Most beats are built from the drums up. A kick on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 gives a steady pulse. Add snare or clap on 5 and 13, then hi-hats on every other step. Once that sounds good, add a bass line and some melody.

The melody instruments (bass, pluck, bell and chord) play different notes depending on which step they're on, so moving a note left or right changes the tune. Experiment! There are no wrong answers.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Make a beat using every instrument.
  • Getting good: Change the tempo and add swing.
  • Expert: Share a beat with a friend.

Did you know?

BPM means beats per minute. Most pop songs are between 100 and 130 BPM, and a resting heart beats about 60 to 100 times a minute.

Questions players ask

How do I save my beat?

Click Copy link. Opening that link brings your beat back exactly as it was.

Why can't I hear anything?

Check the sound button in the top corner isn't muted, and that your device volume is up.
