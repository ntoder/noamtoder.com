About Fishing Frenzy

Sail out to sea and catch as many sea creatures as you can in sixty seconds. Move your boat, drop your hook and reel in whatever bites first. Small fish swim near the top, while octopuses, squid and crabs live deep down and are worth more points. A golden star gives extra time. But watch out: sharks snap your line, and nobody wants an old boot!

How to play

⬅ ➡ / mouseMove the boat
Click / SpaceDrop the hook, then reel in

Tips

  • Wait for the deep sea creatures to swim under your boat before dropping.
  • Reel in early if you see a shark coming.
  • Three good catches in a row double your points.
