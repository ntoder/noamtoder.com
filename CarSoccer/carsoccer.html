Car Soccer

Push the ball into the orange goal on the right! Most goals in 2 minutes wins. In 2-player mode: Blue uses W A S D + Left Shift, Orange uses the arrows + Right Shift.

Get behind the ball before you hit it, lined up with the goal. Then boost straight through it!

About Car Soccer

Football, but with cars. Two cars and one giant ball share a walled pitch. Drive into the ball to knock it around: the faster you're going, the harder you hit it. Boost gives a burst of speed and slowly refills. Score in the opponent's goal as many times as you can in two minutes. Play against the computer or challenge a friend on the same keyboard.

How to play

W / ⬆Drive
S / ⬇Reverse

Tips

  • Hit the ball through its middle to send it straight.
  • Boost refills slowly, so save it for shots.
  • Use the walls: the ball bounces off them.

How to get better at Car Soccer

Car Soccer is about angles. The ball flies away from your car in the direction you hit it, so line up behind the ball with the goal straight ahead before you charge. A glancing hit sends it off to the side.

Boost is your power shot. It makes you much faster, and speed equals shot strength. But it only refills slowly, so don't waste it driving around: save it for when you're lined up.

Defending matters too. When the ball is on your half, get between it and your goal. You can always reverse if you overshoot.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Score a goal.
  • Getting good: Beat the computer.
  • Expert: Win by 5 goals.

Did you know?

Real-world 'car soccer' exists! Some motor shows and festivals hold matches with small cars pushing a huge inflatable ball.

Questions players ask

How do I boost?

Hold Space or Shift. The bar at the top shows how much boost you have.

How long is a match?

Two minutes. Most goals wins.
