About Noam Football

A full eleven-a-side football match with a TV-style camera. Pick one of 16 national teams or build your own team with its own name, kit, badge and player names. Play the computer in a quick match or a three-round knockout cup, or play a friend on the same keyboard. You control one player at a time: pass, play through balls, cross, chip the keeper, beat defenders with skill moves and switch to the teammate closest to the ball. Goalkeepers dive, bad tackles give away free kicks, penalties and cards, every goal gets a slow-motion replay, and drawn cup matches go to a penalty shootout.

Tips

  • Don't hold the ball too long. Quick passes pull defenders out of position.
  • When a striker sprints behind the defence, press W for a through ball.
  • From the wing, press A to cross. Strikers attack the ball in the box and can score with a header.
  • Aim your shot with the arrow keys: hold up or down as you shoot to go for a corner.
  • When defending, hold S to chase the ball carrier, then tap S again to tackle.

How to get better at Noam Football

The fastest way to improve at Noam Football is to stop dribbling into crowds. Computer defenders press the ball carrier and a second defender covers behind, so running straight at goal usually ends in a lost ball. Instead, pass early to a teammate in space with S, and keep moving the ball until a gap opens. Watch the radar at the bottom of the screen: it shows where your teammates are, even when they are off camera.

Most goals come from three moves. First, the through ball: when one of your strikers sprints behind the defence, press W and the pass is played into the space in front of them. Second, the cross: get to the wing near the corner flag and press A, and your strikers will attack the ball in the box and try a header. Third, the shot across the keeper: hold D for power and hold up or down as you release it to aim for a corner. Too much power sends the ball over the bar, so release before the bar turns red.

In defence, patience wins the ball back. Hold S to chase the ball carrier, then tap S again when you are right next to them for a standing tackle. The slide tackle (D) covers more ground, but if you miss, your player is on the floor for a moment and the attacker is through. Press Q to switch to the defender closest to the ball, and try to stay between the ball and your goal.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Play on Amateur with a strong team. Learn to pass with S, shoot with D, and switch players with Q.
  • Getting good: Move up to Pro. Use through balls and crosses, and stop diving into slide tackles.
  • Expert: On Legend the computer reacts quickly and tackles hard. Pass in triangles, play one-twos, and only shoot when you have a clear sight of goal.

Questions players ask

Which player am I controlling?

The one with the yellow arrow and ring. The small bar above shows how much energy they have left for sprinting.

How do I win the Noam Cup?

Choose Noam Cup on the start screen and win three knockout matches in a row: quarter-final, semi-final and final. If a match ends level, it goes to penalties.

How do penalties work?

When you shoot, aim with the arrow keys and hold D for power. When the computer shoots, you're the keeper: press left or right before the kick to dive.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. Drag on the left side of the pitch to run, and use the buttons under the game to pass, shoot, lob and switch players.
