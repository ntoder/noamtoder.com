About Black Hole

A silly, satisfying growing game. You control a black hole that roams around a small town. Everything smaller than the hole falls in: flowers and bins first, then people, bikes and cars. Every swallow makes you a bit bigger, so soon you can gulp down buses, houses, office towers and even castles. Bigger objects give more points. You have 90 seconds to grow as big as you can.

Tips

  • Small things add up fast early on.
  • Objects that are too big wobble when you touch them.
  • The minimap shows where you are in town.

How to get better at Black Hole

Black Hole is about growing efficiently. In the first seconds you can only swallow tiny things, so sweep through patches of flowers, people and bins. Every small thing helps, and soon cars start falling in.

Bigger objects grow you much more than small ones, and they're worth more points. Once something stops wobbling when you touch it, it's small enough: go after all of that kind you can see.

Plan a route. Don't waste time crossing empty roads. Look for crowded blocks and sweep through them in long lines. The minimap helps you see which parts of town you haven't visited yet.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Swallow a car.
  • Getting good: Swallow a house.
  • Expert: Swallow a castle.

Did you know?

Real black holes are places in space where gravity is so strong that not even light can escape. The nearest known one is about 1,500 light-years from Earth.

Questions players ask

Why won't something fall in?

It's bigger than your hole. If it wobbles, grow a bit more and come back.

How do I move on a phone?

Hold your finger on the screen and drag where you want the hole to go.
