About Light Bikes

A neon racing game inspired by sci-fi movies. Four light bikes speed around a dark grid, each leaving a solid wall of light behind it. You can only turn left or right, never stop. Any bike that hits a wall, a trail or another bike is out. Be the last one riding to win the round. Play alone against three computer bikes, or with a friend on the same keyboard.

How to play

Arrow keys or swipeSteer the blue bike
W A S DSteer the orange bike (2 players)

Tips

  • Cut across in front of another bike to box it in.
  • Leave yourself an escape route. Avoid narrow tunnels.
  • The bikes get a little faster every round.

How to get better at Light Bikes

Light Bikes is about space. Every bike is always making walls, so the arena gets smaller and smaller. The safest place is wherever there's the most open room. Avoid driving into a pocket that's closing, because you can't stop or reverse.

To attack, drive alongside another bike and then turn across its path. If you're ahead of it, your new trail becomes a wall it has to dodge. Near the edges this can trap it completely.

When only you and one other bike are left, try to cut the arena in two with your trail and keep the bigger half for yourself. Then ride in tight back-and-forth lines to fill your half slowly, while the other bike runs out of room first.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win a round.
  • Getting good: Win 3 rounds in a row.
  • Expert: Beat a friend in 2-player mode.

Did you know?

The idea of racing bikes that leave walls of light comes from a 1982 science-fiction film about a computer world. It became one of the most copied ideas in video games.

Questions players ask

Can I stop or go backwards?

No. Your bike always moves, and you can only turn left or right.

How does 2-player mode work?

Player 1 is blue with the arrow keys, Player 2 is orange with W A S D. Two computer bikes join in too.
