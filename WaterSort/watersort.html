About Water Sort

A calm colour-sorting puzzle. Each tube holds four layers of coloured water, all mixed up. Tap a tube to pick it up and tap another to pour: the top colour flows over, as much as fits, but only onto the same colour or into an empty tube. Keep pouring until every tube holds just one colour. Levels add more colours as you go, every level is checked by the game to make sure it can be solved, and your progress is saved.

Tips

  • Empty tubes are your most valuable space.
  • Pour the same colour together to free up tubes.
  • Undo as much as you like, there's no penalty.

How to get better at Water Sort

Water Sort is a puzzle about space. The empty tubes are your parking spots: use them to hold a colour while you dig down to what's underneath. But a parked colour blocks that tube, so try to only park when it helps you finish another tube.

Look for colours that are already at the top of several tubes. Pouring them together into one tube both sorts that colour and empties the tops of the others.

If you get stuck, undo a few moves and try a different order. There's also an Extra tube button once per level for really tricky moments.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve 5 levels.
  • Getting good: Solve level 15.
  • Expert: Solve level 30.

Did you know?

Puzzles like this are a kind of sorting problem, the same kind computers solve millions of times a second when they put lists in order.

Questions players ask

Can a level be impossible?

No. The game tests every level with a solver before giving it to you.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, you'll continue from your highest level on this device.
