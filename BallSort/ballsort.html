About Ball Sort

A calm color-sorting puzzle. The balls are mixed up across the tubes, and your job is to sort them so each tube holds just one color. You can only move the top ball, and it can only land on a ball of the same color or in an empty tube. Every level adds another color, so planning ahead matters more and more.

Tips

  • Keep at least one tube empty for as long as you can.
  • Finish one color completely before starting the next.
  • Undo is free, so experiment!

How to get better at Ball Sort

Ball Sort looks simple, but the later levels need planning. The empty tubes are your most valuable tool: they let you park a ball while you dig down to the color you need. Try to keep one tube empty for as long as you can, and only fill it when it finishes a color.

Work on one color at a time. Pick a color that is already near the top of several tubes, gather it into one tube, and then move on. Every completed tube is one less thing to worry about.

If you get stuck, use Undo to step back a few moves instead of restarting. Often a single different move early on opens up the whole puzzle.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Levels 1 to 3 have only a few colors. Learn how parking balls works.
  • Getting good: From level 4, plan two or three moves ahead.
  • Expert: Later levels have many colors and only two empty tubes, so every move counts.

Did you know?

Sorting is one of the most studied problems in computer science. Programs use clever sorting methods like quicksort and merge sort to put millions of items in order in a fraction of a second.

Questions players ask

Can every level be solved?

Yes, there are always two empty tubes, which is enough to sort the balls.

How do I get more points?

Solve each level in fewer moves for a bigger bonus.
