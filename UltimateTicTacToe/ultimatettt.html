About Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

A much deeper version of tic-tac-toe. The board is a big 3×3 grid, and every square of it is a small tic-tac-toe game. Win a small game to claim its square on the big board, and get three big squares in a row to win the whole thing. The clever rule: wherever you play inside a small board, your opponent's next move must be in the matching small board. If that board is already finished, they can play anywhere. Play against the computer or a friend.

Tips

  • Every move is also a choice of where your opponent goes next.
  • Sending your rival to a finished board gives them a free choice: avoid it.
  • The centre board is the most valuable.

How to get better at Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe is about two things at once: winning small boards, and controlling where your opponent plays. Every square you choose sends them to a matching board. Before you move, check that board. Can they win it immediately? Then don't send them there.

Free moves are powerful. If you send your opponent to a board that's already won or full, they can play anywhere, which usually means they'll pick the best spot on the whole board. Try never to give them that.

Like normal tic-tac-toe, the centre board and the corners are the most valuable on the big board. Sometimes it's worth letting your opponent win a side board if it lets you take the centre.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win a small board.
  • Getting good: Beat the computer.
  • Expert: Win against a friend.

Did you know?

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe became popular among maths students in the 2010s. Unlike normal tic-tac-toe, nobody has worked out a perfect strategy for it yet.

Questions players ask

Where can I play?

In the glowing board. It's decided by where your opponent played last. If that board is finished, any open board glows.

What if a small board is a tie?

Nobody gets that big square.
