Time Loop

Move with the arrow keys / WASD or by dragging. Grab every 💎 gem before the 8-second clock runs out.

Your past selves always repeat the same path, so make each run's path simple and easy to avoid later.

About Time Loop

A brain-bending game about getting in your own way. You have eight seconds to collect all the gems. Then time rewinds and you start over with new gems, but a ghost of your previous run appears and does exactly what you did, at exactly the same time. Each loop adds another ghost, so the arena fills with copies of yourself. Plan your paths so future you can still get through. How many loops can you survive?

How to play

Arrows / WASD or dragMove

Tips

  • Every ghost repeats your old run exactly, at the same time.
  • Faint trails show where each ghost will go.
  • Leave the starting circle quickly in a new direction.

How to get better at Time Loop

Time Loop is about thinking of your future self. Every move you make now becomes an obstacle later. Short, simple paths that stay in one part of the arena are easier to dodge than long zig-zags across the whole screen.

All ghosts start in the middle at the same moment you do, then spread out. Leave the start in a different direction from the earlier runs. The faint coloured trails show each ghost's path, so you can see where they're going to be.

Timing matters as much as place. A ghost is only dangerous where it is at that exact second. You can cross an old path safely if the ghost passed there earlier or will get there later.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Survive 3 loops.
  • Getting good: Survive with 6 ghosts.
  • Expert: Reach loop 12.

Did you know?

Stories about time loops, where a day repeats over and over, have been popular for decades. In this game, you're the one creating the loop's obstacles.

Questions players ask

What happens at the end of a loop?

Time rewinds, you go back to the middle, and a ghost replays your last run.

Can I touch a ghost at the very start?

For the first half-second everyone overlaps in the middle, so that's allowed.
