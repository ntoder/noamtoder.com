Knife Throw

Tap (or press Space) to throw a knife at the spinning log. Throw all your knives to break the log and move on!
But don't hit a knife that's already stuck. Hit the red apples for bonus points. Every fifth log is a tricky boss.

Game Over

Wait for a big empty space to spin toward the bottom, then throw. On speed-changing logs, throw when it slows down.

About Knife Throw

A one-tap timing game. A wooden log spins at the top of the screen, and you throw knives at it from below. Each knife sticks where it lands and spins around with the log, so the space fills up fast. Hit an old knife and it's game over. Later logs speed up, slow down and change direction, and every fifth stage is a boss log.

How to play

Tap, click or SpaceThrow a knife

Tips

  • Throw into the biggest gap.
  • Count the beat: logs that change speed follow a rhythm.
  • Apples are worth 25 points.

How to get better at Knife Throw

Knife Throw is all about timing. The knife flies straight up and hits the bottom of the log, so watch the bottom edge. When a big empty space is about to arrive there, throw. With practice you'll learn how early to tap for the speed of the log.

As more knives stick, the gaps shrink. Spread your knives out evenly instead of bunching them up, so there's always room for the last few. Knives that start in the log (on later stages) need extra care.

Boss logs and later stages change speed and even direction. Instead of chasing the log, find the moment it slows down and throw then.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear the first boss log on stage 5.
  • Getting good: Reach stage 10.
  • Expert: Hit every apple on a stage.

Did you know?

Real knife throwing is a sport with world championships. Throwers stand at set distances and count how many times the knife spins in the air before it hits.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

A thrown knife hitting a knife that's already in the log.

What are the boss logs?

Every fifth stage the log is purple, faster and changes speed a lot.
