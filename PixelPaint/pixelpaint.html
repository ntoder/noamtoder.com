About Pixel Paint

A pixel art drawing studio. Choose colours from the palette or pick any colour you like, then draw on the grid one square at a time. Fill big areas with the bucket, use mirror mode to paint both sides at once for perfectly symmetrical pictures, and undo any mistakes. Your drawing is saved in your browser automatically, and you can download it as a picture to keep or share.

Tips

  • Start with the outline in a dark colour, then fill it in.
  • Mirror mode is great for faces, hearts and spaceships.
  • Try the 16 by 16 size first. Small pictures look the most like real pixel art.
