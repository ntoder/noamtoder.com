About Target Rush

An aim-training game for mouse and touch. Round targets pop up all over the screen and slowly shrink before vanishing. Hit them while you can: the centre gives double points, hitting in a row builds a streak multiplier, and gold targets are worth five times as much. At the end, your accuracy gives a bonus, so every missed click costs you. Try the Moving mode, where targets drift around, or Tiny mode for a real challenge.

Tips

  • Aim for the white middle ring for double points.
  • Your streak resets when you miss or a target vanishes.
  • Accuracy bonus = half your score × your accuracy.

How to get better at Target Rush

Target Rush rewards being both quick and careful. Every hit adds to your streak, which multiplies your points, and the centre of each target doubles them. A miss or a vanished target resets the streak.

Don't panic-click. At the end, your score gets a bonus based on your accuracy, up to half your score again. Clicking wildly at empty space throws that away. Move to the target, then click once.

Targets shrink as their timer runs out (the ring around them shows how long is left). Older targets are smaller and harder to hit, so it's usually best to hit the newest, biggest ones first, unless an old one is a gold target.

Questions players ask

What's the accuracy bonus?

At the end you get extra points equal to half your score times your accuracy.

What do the modes change?

Moving makes targets drift around, and Tiny makes them smaller but worth 1.5× points.
