Level Maker

Noametry →

Click or drag on the grid to place things. Scroll with the slider, the mouse wheel or ← →.

How to make a level: pick a tool and click on the grid. Spikes kill, blocks can be landed on (but crash into their sides), and yellow jump pads give a super jump. Press ▶ Test to play it (Space, click or tap to jump, hold to keep jumping). When you beat your own level, the 🚀 Publish button unlocks so other players can find it on the Player levels page. You can also send a 🔗 Share link to a friend without publishing. Your level saves by itself in this browser.
