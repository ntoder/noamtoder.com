About Mirror Twins

A brain-twisting puzzle about doing two things at once. A blue twin and an orange twin stand on two separate boards. Every arrow press moves both: up and down move them together, while left and right move them in opposite directions, like a reflection in a mirror. Each board has its own walls and spikes. The trick is using walls to hold one twin still while the other moves. Get both onto their stars at the same time to solve the puzzle. Levels are made fresh each time and checked by a solver.

How to get better at Mirror Twins

Mirror Twins is all about walls. If both twins always moved together, you could never fix a difference between them. But a twin that bumps into a wall stays put while the other one moves. That's how you line them up.

Remember the mirror: up and down are the same for both, but left and right are opposite. When the blue twin needs to go left and the orange twin also needs to go left, you'll need a wall to block one of them for some of the moves.

Spikes end the attempt, so check both boards before each move. The counter shows how many moves you've made and the shortest possible solution, so try to match it for a perfect solve.

Did you know?

Our brains are good at mirrored movements: try drawing circles with both hands at once, one going clockwise and one going anticlockwise. It's harder than it sounds!
