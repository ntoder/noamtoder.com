About Reaction Test

A simple test of how quickly you react. The screen turns red, then after a random wait it flashes green. Tap as soon as you see green. Your time is measured in milliseconds (thousandths of a second). After five tries you get your average and a rating from sleepy turtle to lightning bolt.

How to play

Tap, click or SpaceReact

Tips

  • Most people score between 200 and 300 ms.
  • A keyboard is often a bit faster than a touchscreen.
  • Try it with friends and compare averages!

How to get better at Reaction Test

Reaction Test has one rule: react, don't predict. The wait before green is random, between about one and four seconds, so trying to guess the timing usually gets you a 'Too early!'. Keep your eyes on the middle of the screen and let your finger move when the colour changes.

Get comfortable. Rest your finger lightly on the Space bar or just above the screen. Being tense actually slows you down a little.

Your average is a better measure than one lucky try. Play a few rounds and watch your best average improve. You'll usually be faster when you're awake and focused, and slower when you're tired.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Average under 350 ms.
  • Getting good: Average under 270 ms.
  • Expert: Get a try under 200 ms.

Did you know?

A message from your eye to your finger travels through nerves at up to about 100 metres per second. Most of the reaction time is actually your brain deciding to move.

Questions players ask

What's a millisecond?

One thousandth of a second. 250 ms is a quarter of a second.

What's a good score?

Under 250 ms is very fast. Most people are around 250 to 300 ms.
