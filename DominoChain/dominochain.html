About Domino Chain

A satisfying chain-reaction puzzle. Each level is a stretch of ground with steps up, steps down and gaps, and a golden bell at the far end. You have a limited box of dominoes. Stand them up wherever you like, then push the first one and watch the chain race along. If every domino reaches the next, the last one rings the bell. Use fewer dominoes for more stars.

How to play

Tips

  • A falling domino reaches almost its own height ahead.
  • Stepping up shortens the reach; stepping down makes it easier.
  • Leftover dominoes earn extra stars.

How to get better at Domino Chain

Domino Chain is about spacing. A falling domino can only knock over the next one if it's within reach, a little less than its own height. Too close together wastes dominoes; too far apart and the chain stops.

Steps change everything. If the next domino stands on higher ground, the falling one hits it lower down and has less reach, so place them closer. Going down a step is easier.

Gaps can be bridged: a domino at the very edge of one platform can fall across a narrow gap and hit one standing on the far edge. If the chain stops, press ⟲ to stand them back up and adjust.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Ring the first bell.
  • Getting good: Get 3 stars on a level.
  • Expert: Finish all 6 levels.

Did you know?

Each domino can knock over another one about 1.5 times bigger. Scientists have shown that a chain of growing dominoes could topple something as tall as a skyscraper.

Questions players ask

How do I remove a domino?

Tap it again.

The chain stopped. What now?

Press ⟲ Stand them up, move a domino closer, and push again.
