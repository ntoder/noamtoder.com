About Balloon Pop

A fast, colourful tapping game. Balloons float up from the bottom of the screen, faster and faster as the clock runs down. Tap them to pop them before they drift away. Small balloons give more points than big ones, golden ones are worth extra and icy ones freeze time for a few seconds. But watch out for the black bomb balloons: popping one knocks five seconds off your clock.

How to play

Tips

  • Smaller balloons are worth more points.
  • Save ice balloons for when the screen is busy.
  • Combos multiply your points up to x5.

How to get better at Balloon Pop

Balloon Pop rewards speed and accuracy together. Each balloon is worth more the smaller it is, and popping quickly in a row builds a combo multiplier, up to five times the points. A steady rhythm of quick pops scores far more than chasing one balloon at a time.

Bombs are the main danger. They look dark with a 💣 on them, and they float up mixed in with the others. Popping one costs five seconds and breaks your combo. When the screen gets busy, take a split second to check before you tap.

Gold ★ balloons rise faster than the others but are worth 50 points, so they're worth chasing. Ice ❄ balloons freeze time for three seconds, which is perfect for clearing a crowded screen and building a big combo.

Did you know?

Party balloons float because they're filled with helium, a gas that is lighter than the air around it. The air pushes the balloon up, the same way water pushes up a beach ball.

Questions players ask

What do the bombs do?

Popping a bomb takes 5 seconds off your time and resets your combo.

How does the combo work?

Pop balloons quickly one after another. Every 4 in a row raises the multiplier, up to x5.
