Five in a Row

Take turns placing stones on the board. The first player to get five stones in a straight line, across, down or diagonally, wins!
You play black and go first.

Game Over

Block the computer when it has three in a row with both ends open. If you wait until four, it's usually too late.

About Five in a Row

A classic strategy game played on a 15 by 15 grid, also known as Gomoku. You and the computer take turns placing stones, and the first to make an unbroken line of five wins. It's easy to learn, but the computer attacks and defends at the same time, so you'll need to spot threats early and build two lines at once to beat it.

Tips

  • An open three (empty at both ends) is a big threat. Block it!
  • Make two threats at once so the computer can only stop one.
  • Play near the middle at the start, where there's room to build.

How to get better at Five in a Row

Five in a Row is all about threats. A line of three with empty spaces at both ends is called an open three, and if you don't block it, it becomes an open four, which can't be stopped. So the first rule is: whenever the computer makes an open three, block one end straight away.

To win, you need to create two threats with a single stone, like an open three and a four at the same time. The computer can only block one of them, and you win with the other. Look for spots where two of your lines cross.

Stay close to your other stones and near the middle of the board. Stones on the edge have less room to grow into lines of five.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win your first game by blocking every open three.
  • Getting good: Try to win in fewer than 30 moves.
  • Expert: Set up a double threat on purpose.

Did you know?

Gomoku has been played in Japan for hundreds of years on the same board as the game Go. Computer scientists proved in 1993 that on a 15 by 15 board, the first player can always win with perfect play.

Questions players ask

Who goes first?

You do, with black stones.

Do six in a row count?

Yes, any line of five or more wins.
