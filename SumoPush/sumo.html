Sumo Push

You're the blue wrestler. Move with W A S D (or the arrows) and press Space for a big charge. Push the other wrestler out of the ring!
First to win 3 rounds is the champion. If a round lasts too long, the ring starts shrinking. In 2-player mode, Red uses the arrows and Right Shift.

Game Over

Don't charge from far away: if you miss, you might fly out yourself! Charge when you're close.

About Sumo Push

A pushy, bouncy sumo game. Two round wrestlers face off in a sandy ring. Move around, bump into your opponent, and shove them over the edge. Your charge gives a huge burst of speed and makes your next bump much stronger, but it needs a moment to recharge, shown by the yellow bar. Win three rounds to become the champion. If a round drags on, the ring slowly shrinks.

Tips

  • Stay near the middle of the ring.
  • A charge is strongest at close range.
  • Dodge sideways when your rival charges.

How to get better at Sumo Push

Sumo Push is about position. The wrestler closer to the centre of the ring has the advantage, because they have room to be pushed without falling out. Try to circle around your opponent so that they're between you and the edge.

The charge makes your next bump much stronger, but it also sends you flying. If you charge and miss, you might shoot right out of the ring. Charge only when you're close and lined up.

When your opponent charges, step to the side. They'll fly past you, often toward the edge, and that's your chance to finish them off.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win a round.
  • Getting good: Beat the computer.
  • Expert: Win 3–0.

Did you know?

In real sumo wrestling, a match is often over in a few seconds. You lose if you step out of the ring or if any part of your body other than your feet touches the ground.

Questions players ask

How do I charge?

Press Space (Player 1) or Right Shift (Player 2) while moving.

Why is the ring getting smaller?

After 12 seconds the ring shrinks so rounds don't last forever.
