🎮 Start a new game

Pick a game to start from. Then change the code and watch your game change. Look for the comments that say TRY for ideas.

🚀 Publish to Made by Players

Your game will appear in the Made by Players arcade with your name, after Noam checks it. That usually takes a day or two.

Please don't put your real name, address, school or photos in your game. Games with rude words or that copy someone else's game won't be approved.
