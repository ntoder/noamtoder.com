About Bomb Maze

A maze-blasting action game. You're in a grid full of stone pillars and wooden crates, with robots wandering around. Drop a bomb and it explodes a couple of seconds later in a cross shape, smashing crates and zapping any robot caught in it. Crates hide power-ups that give you more bombs, bigger blasts and faster feet. Red robots are smart and chase you. Clear all the robots to move to the next level.

How to play

Arrows or WASDWalk

Tips

  • Blasts stop at stone pillars, so hide behind one.
  • Your own bomb can hurt you!
  • Red robots chase you; orange ones wander.

How to get better at Bomb Maze

In Bomb Maze, the key move is 'drop and hide'. Bombs explode in a straight cross, so after you drop one, walk around a corner or behind a stone pillar. You're safe as long as you're not in a straight line from the bomb.

Clear crates early to collect power-ups. More bombs let you trap robots between two blasts, and a bigger blast range reaches robots from further away. Speed helps you escape, but be careful not to run into your own blast.

Robots move along the corridors. Drop a bomb where a robot is heading, then step away. Red robots follow you, which makes them easier to lure into a trap: let them chase you past a bomb you've just dropped.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear level 1.
  • Getting good: Collect 3 power-ups.
  • Expert: Reach level 5.

Did you know?

This style of maze-bombing game started on home computers in the 1980s and became one of the most popular party games for four players.

Questions players ask

Can my own bomb hurt me?

Yes! Always step out of the cross shape before it goes off.

How do I finish a level?

Knock out every robot.
