Mancala

Pick a pit on your side (the bottom row). Its stones are dropped one by one into the next pits, going right and around the board.
Land your last stone in your store (the big pit on the right) to go again. Land it in an empty pit on your side to capture the stones across from it!

About Mancala

One of the oldest board games in the world, played here with the popular Kalah rules. Each player has six pits and a store. On your turn you scoop up all the stones from one of your pits and sow them one at a time around the board. Free turns and captures make every move a little puzzle, and whoever has the most stones in their store at the end wins.

Tips

  • Look for pits that land exactly in your store for a free turn.
  • Empty pits on your side are capture traps.
  • Watch the computer's side: don't leave a big pile where it can be captured.

How to get better at Mancala

The best move in Mancala is often the free turn. If a pit has exactly as many stones as it is away from your store, your last stone lands in the store and you play again. Count from the right: the rightmost pit needs one stone, the next needs two, and so on. Chaining free turns is how strong players race ahead.

Captures are the other big source of points. When your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you take it plus everything in the pit directly across. So keep an eye on empty pits and on big piles on the computer's side.

Defence matters too. Before you move, check whether you're leaving a big pile across from an empty pit on the computer's side. If you are, the computer may capture it on its next turn.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win by using free turns whenever you see one.
  • Getting good: Start setting up captures with empty pits.
  • Expert: Protect your big piles and think a move ahead.

Did you know?

Mancala-type games have been played for over a thousand years across Africa and Asia. There are hundreds of local versions with different numbers of pits and rules.

Questions players ask

What happens at the end?

When one side has no stones left, the other player puts all their remaining stones in their own store.

Do stones go into the computer's store?

No, you skip the other player's store when you sow.
