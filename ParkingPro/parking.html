About Parking Pro

A careful-driving puzzle. Each level gives you a car and a glowing parking spot somewhere in a busy car park. Drive and steer like a real car (it can't turn on the spot) and slide into the space between the other cars. The car must be completely inside the lines, lined up straight and stopped. Six levels take you from an easy empty lot to tight parallel parking. Park fast for three stars.

Tips

  • Go slowly. You can always reverse and try again.
  • Reversing into a space is often easier.
  • You can be facing either way in the spot.

How to get better at Parking Pro

Parking Pro uses real car steering: the car only turns while it's moving, and the back wheels follow a tighter curve than the front. That means the back of the car swings in, so give corners extra room.

Slow is smooth. Tap the arrow keys instead of holding them, and use Space to brake. A crash sends you back to the start of the level, which costs much more time than going carefully.

For parallel parking, drive past the gap, stop, then reverse while steering towards the space. When the back of the car is in, steer the other way to straighten up. It's exactly how real drivers do it.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Park in the first 3 levels.
  • Getting good: Earn 3 stars on any level.
  • Expert: Finish all 6 levels.

Did you know?

Some modern cars can park themselves. They use sensors to measure the space and steer automatically, while the driver controls the speed.

Questions players ask

Why doesn't it count as parked?

The whole car must be inside the green spot, almost straight, and stopped for a second.

Can I park facing backwards?

Yes, either direction counts.
