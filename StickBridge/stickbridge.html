About Stick Bridge

A simple game about guessing distances. Your little ninja stands on a pillar, and the next pillar is somewhere across a gap. Hold down to grow a stick, then let go. The stick tips over and becomes a bridge. If it lands on the next pillar, your ninja walks across. If it's too short or too long, down they go. Hitting the red spot in the middle of a pillar gives extra points, and more for every perfect hit in a row.

Tips

  • The stick grows at the same speed every time, so you can learn to count.
  • Narrow pillars are harder. Aim for the middle.
  • Perfect hits in a row are worth more and more.

How to get better at Stick Bridge

Stick Bridge is a game of judging distance. The stick always grows at the same speed, so the length depends only on how long you hold. Some players count in their head, others watch the stick top and imagine it falling over onto the gap.

The stick falls from the edge of your pillar, so the length you need is the gap plus part of the next pillar. Aim for the middle of the next pillar, not its near edge, to give yourself room for error both ways.

The red spot in the middle gives bonus points, and a chain of perfect hits adds more each time. If you're going for a high score, take the time to aim. If you just want to survive, aim for the middle anyway since that's the safest place.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Cross 10 gaps.
  • Getting good: Score 25 points.
  • Expert: Hit 3 perfect spots in a row.

Did you know?

Judging distance is called depth perception. Our two eyes see from slightly different places, and the brain uses that difference to work out how far away things are.

Questions players ask

What happens if the stick is too long?

How do I get bonus points?

Make the stick end land on the red spot in the middle of the pillar.
