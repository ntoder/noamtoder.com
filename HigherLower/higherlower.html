About Higher or Lower

A classic card guessing game. One card is face up. Guess whether the next card from the deck will be higher or lower. If you're right, your streak grows and each win is worth more than the last. If you're wrong, you lose a life and the streak starts over. A card with the same value counts as a win. The deck is a normal 52-card deck, so if you remember which cards have gone, you can play the odds.

Tips

  • Middle cards (6, 7, 8) are the riskiest.
  • Matching values count as a win.
  • The recent cards row helps you remember what's already been played.

How to get better at Higher or Lower

Higher or Lower is a game of probability. With an Ace showing, almost every card is higher. With a King, almost every card is lower. These are easy points. The hard ones are in the middle: on a 7, there are about as many higher cards as lower ones.

The deck doesn't refill until it runs out, so cards that have been played can't come up again. If you've already seen lots of high cards, the next card is more likely to be low. The row of recent cards at the bottom helps you keep track.

Your streak multiplies your points, so one wrong guess on a 7 can cost you a big streak. There's no way to skip a card, so go with the side that has more cards left, even if it's only slightly more.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win 5 in a row.
  • Getting good: Score 300 points.
  • Expert: Win 10 in a row.

Did you know?

There are 52 cards in a normal deck. If a 7 is showing, 24 of the other 51 are higher, 24 are lower, and 3 are also sevens.
