About Pinball

A neon pinball table. Launch the ball with the plunger, then keep it in play with the two flippers at the bottom. Round bumpers kick the ball away for 100 points each, the pink slingshots fire it across the table, green drop targets on the left build up your multiplier, and the three lights at the top give a big bonus when you light them all. You have three balls.

Tips

  • The multiplier multiplies every point you score.
  • Losing a ball resets the multiplier.
  • A full plunger pull shoots the ball all the way to the top lanes.

How to get better at Pinball

In pinball, control beats speed. When the ball comes down toward a flipper, you can hold the flipper up and let the ball roll onto it and stop. This is called a 'cradle'. From there you can aim a careful shot instead of a random flick.

Where the ball hits the flipper decides where it goes. Hitting near the tip sends it across the table to the other side; hitting near the base sends it higher up the same side. Practise aiming at the green targets on the left to build your multiplier.

Don't flip both flippers at once all the time, because it leaves a gap in the middle. Watch the ball and flip only the one it's heading for.

Did you know?

Pinball machines were banned in several big American cities from the 1940s to the 1970s, because people thought they were gambling. Flippers were added in 1947 to prove they needed skill.

Questions players ask

How do I launch the ball?

Hold ⬇ or Space to pull the plunger back, then let go. Longer pull = stronger launch.

How does the multiplier work?

Knock down all four green targets to add +1 to your multiplier, up to x5. Losing a ball resets it.
