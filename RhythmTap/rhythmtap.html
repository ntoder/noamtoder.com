About Rhythm Tap

A music rhythm game. Notes slide down four coloured lanes in time with the beat, and you tap each one just as it reaches the line. Perfect timing scores the most, and building a long combo multiplies your points up to four times. Choose from three songs with different speeds, from a gentle warm-up to a fast challenge, and aim for an S grade.

How to play

D F J KHit the four lanes
Arrow keysAlso hit the lanes
Tap a laneHit it on a touch screen

Tips

  • Listen to the beat. The notes follow the music.
  • Rest your fingers on D F J K, like typing.
  • Don't press early. Wait for the note to touch the line.
