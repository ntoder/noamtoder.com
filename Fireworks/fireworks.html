About Fireworks

A calm creative toy for when you just want to make something pretty. Tap the night sky over the city and a rocket shoots up and bursts right where you tapped. Choose from six shapes, including peony, ring, heart, star, golden willow and crackle, and pick a colour or let the rainbow choose. Press Big show for an automatic display.

How to play

Tap or click the skyLaunch a firework
SpaceLaunch a random one

Tips

  • Willows fall slowly like golden rain.
  • Crackles pop again as they fade.
  • Tap lots of times quickly for a grand finale.

How to get better at Fireworks

Fireworks has no way to win or lose. It's a toy for relaxing and making something beautiful. Tap anywhere in the sky and a firework will fly up and burst exactly there.

Mix shapes and colours to make your own show. Real fireworks shows often start with single bursts, build up with rings and stars, and end with a fast finale of many rockets at once.

If you want to just watch, press Big show and the sky fills with random fireworks for a few seconds.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Launch 20 fireworks.
  • Getting good: Make a heart in every colour.
  • Expert: Launch 100 fireworks.

Did you know?

Firework colours come from metals: strontium makes red, copper makes blue, sodium makes yellow and barium makes green.

Questions players ask

Is there a score?

No. Fireworks is a relaxing toy. The counter just shows how many you've launched.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes, use the colour buttons under the sky. The rainbow button picks a random colour each time.
