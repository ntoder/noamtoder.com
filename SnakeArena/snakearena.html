About Snake Arena

A slithery battle in a giant round arena. You start as a tiny snake among fifteen computer snakes. Eat the glowing dots to grow longer. If your head touches another snake's body you're out, but if another snake runs into you, it bursts into a pile of food you can gobble up. Hold boost to speed up, but boosting costs you some length. Try to climb the live leaderboard to number one.

Tips

  • Stay away from other snakes' heads while you're small.
  • Boost across in front of a snake to make it crash into your body.
  • Circle around a smaller snake to trap it.
  • Boosting makes you shorter, so only use it when it counts.
  • The red edge of the arena is deadly.

How to get better at Snake Arena

In Snake Arena, the rule that matters most is simple: it's the head that dies. If your head touches any part of another snake's body, you're out, but your own body is a weapon. That means the best way to grow is to make other snakes crash into you, then eat the big pile of food they leave behind.

While you're small, play safe. Eat the dots in quiet areas, stay away from big snakes' heads, and keep away from the red edge. Once you're longer, you can start hunting. Boost to get in front of a smaller snake's head, so it has nowhere to turn but into your side. Bigger snakes can even circle all the way around a small one to trap it.

Boosting is powerful but costs you length, so use it in short bursts: to escape, to cut someone off, or to reach food from a snake that just crashed. Watch the minimap in the corner to see where the big snakes are, and the leaderboard to see who you need to beat.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Eat dots and avoid every other snake. Get to a length of 100.
  • Getting good: Start using boost to grab the food when a snake crashes nearby.
  • Expert: Hunt: cut in front of smaller snakes and try to reach number one on the leaderboard.

Did you know?

Real snakes don't have eyelids. Instead, each eye is covered by a clear scale that protects it, which is why snakes look like they never blink.

Questions players ask

Why did I lose?

Your head touched another snake's body, or the red wall at the edge of the arena.

What does boost do?

It makes you go much faster, but you slowly lose length while boosting.

Are the other snakes real players?

No, they're computer snakes with their own names. Some just eat, and some like to hunt!

How is my score worked out?

Your longest length times 10, plus 100 points for every snake that crashed into you.
