About Archery

A bow-and-arrow skill game. Drag back from anywhere on the screen to draw the bow: the further you pull, the harder the shot. The arrow flies in a curve because of gravity, and the wind pushes it along. Hit the target as close to the yellow centre as you can. The targets move further away, get smaller, and later even slide up and down.

How to play

Tips

  • A long pull gives a flatter, faster arrow.
  • Headwind (⬅) makes the arrow drop short; tailwind (➡) carries it further.
  • On moving targets, shoot where the target will be.

How to get better at Archery

Archery is about finding the right power and angle. Pull back further for a faster, flatter shot. A strong pull at a low angle is easier to aim than a weak pull lobbed high, because a fast arrow has less time to be pushed by gravity and wind.

The dotted line shows the start of the arrow's path, but not the whole flight. Remember how your last arrow landed and adjust from there: if it hit low, aim a little higher next time.

Wind pushes the arrow along its flight. A headwind (pointing left) slows it so it drops sooner, and a tailwind (pointing right) carries it further. From round 5 targets move up and down, so time your release for when the target will be at the right height.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Hit a bullseye.
  • Getting good: Score 60 points.
  • Expert: Score 85 points.

Did you know?

Olympic archers shoot at a target 70 metres away. Its centre ring, worth 10 points, is just 12.2 centimetres across, about the size of a CD.

Questions players ask

How do I shoot?

Press anywhere, drag backwards (away from the target), and let go.

What does the wind do?

It pushes the arrow forward or back while it flies, changing how far it goes.
