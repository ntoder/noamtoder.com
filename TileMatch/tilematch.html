About Tile Match

A cute stacking puzzle with fruit, flowers, cars and more. The tiles are piled up in layers, and you can only take the ones on top. Tap a tile to send it to the tray, and whenever three matching tiles meet in the tray, they vanish. Clear every tile to win the level, but if the tray fills up with seven tiles, the game is over.

How to play

Click or tap a tileMove it to the tray

Tips

  • Grab tiles that finish a pair in the tray first.
  • Uncover tiles under the stack before the tray gets crowded.
  • Save the Shuffle for when you're stuck.

How to get better at Tile Match

The tray is your most important resource. Every different kind of tile you put in it takes up space until you find its two partners. Try to keep no more than two or three kinds in the tray at the same time.

Before tapping, look at what's already in the tray and search the board for matching tiles that are free. Completing a set clears three spaces at once. If no match is free, pick a tile that uncovers lots of tiles underneath.

Use Undo when a move goes wrong, and keep the Shuffle for emergencies, when none of the free tiles match anything.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Clear level 1 without using Undo.
  • Getting good: Reach level 4 with more layers.
  • Expert: Beat level 8 with a full stack of icons.

Did you know?

Tile-matching games go back to the old Chinese game of mahjong, which uses 144 tiles. Mahjong solitaire, where you match pairs of tiles from a pile, became a computer game in 1981.

Questions players ask

Why can't I pick some tiles?

Greyed-out tiles are covered by tiles above them. Clear the ones on top first.

What does Shuffle do?

It swaps the pictures on the tiles left on the board. You get one per level.
