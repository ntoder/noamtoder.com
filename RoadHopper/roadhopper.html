About Road Hopper

An endless hopping adventure. Every hop forward takes you to a new lane: busy roads full of cars and trucks, rivers where you have to ride floating logs or jump across lily pads, and train tracks where a flashing light warns that a train is coming. Grab coins along the way and spend them on new characters like a frog, a cat, a penguin, a robot and a unicorn. Keep moving, because if you wait too long an eagle swoops down!

How to play

Arrow keys or WASDHop forward, back, left or right
PhoneTap to hop forward, swipe to hop sideways or back

Tips

  • Watch the red light on the train tracks. When it flashes, a train is coming.
  • On rivers, logs carry you sideways, so don't ride one off the edge of the screen.
  • You can hop backward to dodge a car.
  • Coins on the road are risky. Only grab them when the lane is clear.
  • Don't wait too long in one spot, or the eagle gets you.

How to get better at Road Hopper

Road Hopper is about timing, not speed. Before each road, look at the cars in the lane you're about to enter and wait for a gap that's bigger than one car. Hopping takes a moment, so you need to be moving into the gap as the last car passes, not after. Remember that you can also hop backward or sideways to get out of trouble.

Rivers work the opposite way: the water is deadly, so you need something to stand on. Wait until a log is right in front of you, then hop on and let it carry you. Logs drift sideways, so keep an eye on the edges of the screen and hop forward again before you float away. Lily-pad rivers stay still, so just pick a path from pad to pad.

Train tracks look empty most of the time, which is what makes them dangerous. When the red light at the side starts flashing, a train is about to rush through, so wait for it. And don't stop for too long anywhere: the screen keeps moving forward, and if you fall behind or stand still, the eagle swoops in.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Get to 25 hops. Practise waiting for gaps in the traffic.
  • Getting good: Get to 75. Learn to ride the logs and watch for train lights.
  • Expert: Beat 150. The roads and rivers get faster the further you go.

Did you know?

The first video games about crossing busy roads came out in the early 1980s. Real road-crossing safety lessons for kids use the same rule the game does: stop, look both ways, and wait for a big enough gap.

Questions players ask

How do I unlock characters?

Collect coins while you play. On the start or game-over screen, tap a locked character to buy it with your coins.

Why did an eagle grab me?

You stood still for too long, or the screen moved past you. Keep hopping forward!

Can I swim?

No, falling in the water ends the game. Stand on logs or lily pads.

Does the game get harder?

Yes. Cars, logs and rivers get faster the further you go.
