About Crazy Eights

A classic card game for the whole family. Everyone starts with seven cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the pile by suit or by number. Eights are wild: you can play one on anything and pick the suit the next player must follow. If you can't play, draw from the deck. The first player to get rid of every card wins the round. Play against one, two or three computer opponents.

How to get better at Crazy Eights

Crazy Eights is a race to empty your hand. Each turn, play a card that matches the top card's suit or number. Matching by number is a great way to switch to a suit you have more of.

Eights are your most powerful cards because you can play them on anything. Don't waste them early. Keep them for when you'd otherwise have to draw, or to finish the game.

When you play an 8, choose the suit you hold the most of. That gives you the best chance of playing again next turn. Also watch your opponents: if one of them has only one or two cards left, try to switch the suit away from what they last played.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Win a game against 1 computer player.
  • Getting good: Win against 3 computer players.
  • Expert: Win 5 games.

Did you know?

Crazy Eights became popular in the 1930s and later inspired many famous shop-bought card games that use special wild cards.

Questions players ask

What are 8s for?

An 8 can be played on any card, and you get to choose the next suit.

What if I can't play?

Tap the deck to draw. After three draws without a match, your turn passes.
