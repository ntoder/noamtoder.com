About Box Pusher

A classic warehouse puzzle. You're a little worker in a maze of walls, and your job is to push every crate onto a gold target spot. You can push a crate if there's free floor behind it, but you can never pull one, and you can't push two at once. That means one wrong push can trap a crate for good. Plan ahead, use undo freely, and work through ten levels of growing difficulty. Your progress is saved.

Tips

  • A crate in a corner is stuck forever (unless that's a target).
  • A crate against a wall can only slide along that wall.
  • Work out which crate goes on which target before you start.

How to get better at Box Pusher

Box Pusher is about avoiding dead ends. Because you can only push, a crate pushed into a corner can never move again. A crate pushed against a wall can only slide along that wall. Before every push, ask yourself: can I still get this crate to a target from there?

Plan the order. Usually the targets furthest inside the room need to be filled first, otherwise the crates you placed earlier block the way. Look at the level for a while before you move.

Undo is your friend. Press Z as many times as you like to step back. There's no penalty, so experiment, and if a level gets really tangled, press R to start it fresh.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve level 3.
  • Getting good: Solve level 6.
  • Expert: Solve all 10 levels.

Did you know?

This kind of puzzle is called Sokoban, which means 'warehouse keeper' in Japanese. The first version was made in Japan in 1981.

Questions players ask

Can I pull a crate?

No, only push. That's what makes it tricky!

Is my progress saved?

Yes, the game remembers the furthest level you've reached on this device.
