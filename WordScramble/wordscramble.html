About Word Scramble

A fast word game against the clock. Each round shows the letters of an English word in a jumbled order, and you rebuild the word by tapping the letters or typing them. Solve as many as you can in 90 seconds, and every correct word buys you a little more time. Words start at five letters and get longer as you go.

How to play

Tips

  • Find the vowels first and think about where they go.
  • Many words end in -ER, -ET, -LE or -ON.
  • Skip a word if you're stuck: it saves time.

How to get better at Word Scramble

Speed comes from spotting patterns. Instead of trying every order, look for letter pairs that often go together in English, like TH, CH, SH, ER, LE and ON. Once you've found a likely pair or ending, the rest of the word often falls into place.

Look at where the vowels could go. Most five- and six-letter words follow simple shapes, like consonant-vowel-consonant. If there are two vowels, they're often split up.

Don't get stuck. You have three skips, and a skipped word shows you the answer, so you'll learn it for next time. Every solved word adds three seconds, so a quick skip can earn you more points than staring at a hard one.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Solve 5 words in one game.
  • Getting good: Solve 12 words.
  • Expert: Score over 600 points.

Did you know?

Puzzles made from rearranged letters are called anagrams. Some people make anagrams of their own names just for fun.

Questions players ask

Are the words in English?

Yes, they're common English words.

Do longer words give more points?

Yes, each letter is worth 10 points, plus a bonus for time left.
