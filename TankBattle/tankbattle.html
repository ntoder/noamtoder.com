About Tank Battle

Drive your tank around a battlefield of brick and steel walls and protect your base from waves of enemy tanks. Shoot through brick to make new paths, hide in the bushes, and grab power-ups for stronger shots, a shield, a bomb that destroys every enemy, or a clock that freezes them. Fast tanks and heavy armoured tanks join in on later levels. Destroy them all to move on to the next battlefield.

How to play

Arrow keys / WASDDrive
SpaceFire

Tips

  • Never leave your base unguarded for too long.
  • Line up with enemy tanks and shoot first.
  • Save the bomb power-up for when lots of enemies are on screen.
