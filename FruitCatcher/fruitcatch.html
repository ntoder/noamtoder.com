About Fruit Catcher

A cheerful catching game for all ages. Fruit tumbles down from the trees and you slide your basket left and right to catch it. Catch ten in a row and your points multiply. Stars are worth extra, the turtle puts everything in slow motion, and the big basket makes catching easier for a while. But bombs, old boots and bones fall too, and every one you catch costs a heart. It gets faster the longer you play.

How to play

Mouse, drag or ⬅ ➡Move the basket

Tips

  • Catching 10 in a row starts a combo multiplier.
  • Missing a fruit breaks your combo.
  • Grab 🐢 when it gets too fast.

How to get better at Fruit Catcher

Fruit Catcher is about keeping your eyes up. Look at what's falling high on the screen, not just what's about to land, so you can plan where to move next.

Your combo multiplier grows with every ten fruits in a row, up to five times the points. Missing a fruit resets it, so it's worth stretching for every one, unless a bomb is in the way.

Junk is the real danger. If a boot is falling right next to an apple, let the apple go. Losing a combo is better than losing a heart.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Score 300 points.
  • Getting good: Reach a x3 combo.
  • Expert: Score 2,000 points.

Did you know?

Apples float in water because about a quarter of an apple is air. That's why bobbing for apples works!

Questions players ask

What costs a life?

Catching a bomb, a boot or a bone.

What do the 🐢 and 🧺 do?

The turtle slows everything down for 5 seconds; the basket makes yours bigger for 8 seconds.
