Weather Wizard

You're a weather wizard looking after a garden. Pick a spell below the sky, then tap above a plant to cast it.
🌧️ Rain waters plants and puts out fires. ☀️ Sun gives them light. 💨 Wind blows away bug swarms. Each plant needs both bars (💧 blue, ☀️ yellow) between the white marks to grow. Get 6 flowers blooming to finish the day. Lightning starts fires, so put them out fast!

Game Over

Too much rain or sun is as bad as too little. Watch the bars under each plant and keep them between the white lines.

About Weather Wizard

A weather-magic strategy game. A row of little plants needs your help to bloom. Each one has a water bar and a sunlight bar, and only grows when both are in the healthy zone. Cast rain clouds and sunbeams to keep them happy, but your magic meter is limited and refills slowly. Meanwhile, lightning strikes start fires that spread from plant to plant, and from day 2 swarms of bugs come to nibble your garden. Use rain to put out fires and wind to blow the bugs away. Bloom six flowers to see the next, harder day.

Tips

  • Rain and sun each cover about two plants.
  • Fires spread to neighbours: stop them early!
  • Your magic refills slowly, so don't waste it.

How to get better at Weather Wizard

Weather Wizard is a balancing act. Every plant has two bars: water (blue) and sun (yellow). A plant only grows when both are between the white marks. Too little and it wilts; too much and it stops growing. Water slowly dries up and sunshine slowly fades, so you'll need to keep coming back.

Each spell covers a patch of about two plants, so aim between two plants that both need the same thing. Magic is limited and refills slowly, so don't cast on plants that are already happy.

Emergencies come first. A fire burns a plant's growth away and spreads to its neighbours, so rain on it as soon as you see the flames. From day 2, bug swarms eat your plants. One gust of wind blows them away.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Finish day 1.
  • Getting good: Reach day 3.
  • Expert: Reach day 6.

Did you know?

A single thunderstorm can drop millions of litres of water. Lightning is about five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.

Questions players ask

Why isn't my plant growing?

Both its water and sun bars must be between the white marks.

How do I put out a fire?

Cast rain on it.
