About Turbo Kart

A top-down kart racer. You and three computer drivers line up on the grid, the lights count down, and it's three laps to the finish. The road is grey with red-and-white kerbs; the grass slows you right down. Yellow arrow pads give you a short turbo boost. Bump other karts out of the way, take the racing line, and try to cross the line first. The track changes every race.

Tips

  • Take the inside of corners for a shorter route.
  • Boost arrows give 1.2 seconds of extra speed.
  • The computer karts aren't perfect: catch them on the corners.

How to get better at Turbo Kart

Turbo Kart is about keeping your speed up. The grass cuts your top speed by more than half, so going off the road costs a lot of time. On sharp corners, ease off the accelerator for a moment before turning, then speed up again on the way out.

The racing line helps. Approach a corner from the outside, cut to the inside at the middle of the bend, and drift back out as you leave it. It's the smoothest and fastest path.

Boost arrows are placed on straights. Steer to drive right over them. A boost at the start of a straight lasts all the way down it, which is a great way to overtake.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Finish a race.
  • Getting good: Finish on the podium (top 3).
  • Expert: Win a race.

Did you know?

Go-karts were invented in 1956 in California by Art Ingels, who built one from scrap metal and a lawnmower engine.

Questions players ask

How do I boost?

Drive over the yellow arrows on the road.

Are there different tracks?

Yes, the track changes each race.
