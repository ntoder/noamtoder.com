About Piano Tiles

A fast tapping game that turns your taps into music. Black tiles scroll down the screen in four columns. Tap each black tile in order and it plays the next note of a tune. Tap a white space or let a black tile reach the bottom and the game ends. The tiles get faster the longer you last.

Tips

  • Use two thumbs, or four fingers on D F J K.
  • Look ahead at the next tile, not the one you're tapping.
  • The speed keeps rising, so stay relaxed.

How to get better at Piano Tiles

Piano Tiles is about rhythm and reading ahead. Only the lowest black tile counts, so tap it, then move your eyes straight to the next one. Players who look at the tile they're tapping are always one step behind.

On a keyboard, rest your fingers on D, F, J and K like a piano player. On a phone, use both thumbs: left thumb for the two left columns, right for the two right ones.

The speed rises a little with every tile. When it gets fast, don't panic and tap harder. Small, quick taps are more accurate than big ones.

From beginner to expert

  • Beginner: Tap 30 tiles.
  • Getting good: Tap 100 tiles.
  • Expert: Get past 200 tiles.

Did you know?

A real piano has 88 keys: 52 white and 36 black. The black keys are grouped in twos and threes so pianists can find their place without looking.

Questions players ask

What ends the game?

Tapping a white space, or letting a black tile go past the bottom.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes: D, F, J and K (or 1 to 4) are the four columns.
